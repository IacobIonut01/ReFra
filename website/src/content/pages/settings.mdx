ReFra keeps all of its options under one hub: open **Library** and tap the **gear icon** at the top right. This page maps each section to what it controls and to the guides that cover it.

## App settings vs Android settings

Two different places can affect what ReFra does:

- **In-app settings**: everything in the table above. These only change how ReFra itself behaves.
- **Android's app settings**: system-level switches like media permission, notifications, battery, and default apps. Reach them from your phone's Settings › Apps › ReFra, or through the **Open app settings** button the app offers when a permission was refused. See [permissions](/ReFra/features/permissions).

If a feature is missing entirely rather than misconfigured (no favorites, no trash, no cloud section), it is usually a permission, a device capability, or the build type; the [requirements](/ReFra/requirements) page lists the gates.

## Example: stop the bars from hiding

1. Open **Library** and tap the gear icon.
2. Tap **Navigation** and switch off **Hide navigation bar on scroll**.
3. Go back, open **Timeline & albums › Top bar**, and switch off **Hide on scroll**.
4. Back on the timeline, both bars stay put while you scroll.

## If you cannot find an option

- Try the search field in the app's built-in **Help & tips**; it links into the relevant settings screen.
- Options that belong to a feature (grid size, story cards, selection actions) sit under the section named after it, not under General.
- Some rows only appear when their feature exists: the Cloud Accounts section shows only on builds that ship cloud providers, and the trash options need Android 11 or later. The Smart Features section is always there; downloading the models happens inside it.
