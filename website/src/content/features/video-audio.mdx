The **Audio** button on the video editor's toolbar opens the audio tools. Each source plays on its own lane below the video track, and the mix goes into the exported copy.

## Add audio

<PreviewCollection feature="video-audio" section="add-audio" />

**Add audio** offers three sources:

- **Files** imports an audio file onto a new lane at the playhead.
- **Record** records a voice-over onto a new lane, starting at the playhead.
- **Extract from video** moves the selected clip's sound to its own lane so you can edit it separately.

With **Fill empty audio tracks** on in the editor's settings, new audio goes into the selected empty track instead of a new one.

## Voice-over

**Voice-over** records from the microphone. **Input** names the microphone in use. Set a **Count-in**, and turn on **Monitor in headphones** to hear yourself while recording; use headphones to avoid feedback. The **Input level** meter shows the signal. **Start recording** begins after the count-in, **Stop** keeps the take, and **Cancel** throws it away.

The first time, the editor explains why it needs the microphone before Android asks. The recording stays on the device. **Microphone** in the editor's settings shows whether access is allowed.

## Clip audio

**Clip audio** edits the selected audio clip: **Gain**, **Pan**, **Mute**, and **Fade in** and **Fade out** times. For a video clip's own sound, **Linked to video** shows whether it plays with the clip or on its own lane.

## Mixer

<PreviewCollection feature="video-audio" section="mixer" />

The **Mixer** has a fader for each lane, the **Source sound** of the main video, and the **Master** output. Each strip can be muted, and audio lanes can also be soloed. **Limiter** puts a hard ceiling on the master output so loud passages do not clip.

## Routing, ducking, and EQ

These tools work on the selected audio lane:

- **Routing** (**Channels & routing**): the **Left channel** and **Right channel** gains, **Link L/R** to move both together, and **Mono downmix**, which blends both channels into one before gain and pan. The lane plays to the master stereo output.
- **Ducking** lowers this lane while another lane plays, such as music under a voice-over. Choose the lane to listen to, then set **Reduction**, **Attack**, and **Release**; **Preview mix** plays the result. Ducking needs at least two audio lanes.
- **EQ** shapes the tone with **Low**, **Mid**, and **High** bands and a **High-pass** filter, with **Flat** and **Voice clarity** presets. **Bypass EQ** passes the lane through untouched.

## Example: music under a voice-over

1. Tap **Audio**, then **Add audio › Files**, and pick a music track.
2. Move the playhead to the start and record a **Voice-over**.
3. Select the music lane, open **Ducking**, and listen to the voice-over lane.
4. Set **Reduction** so the voice stays clear, then check it with **Preview mix**.
