Four tools in the **Adjust** group change brightness and colour. Each one opens over the photo with its controls at the bottom; **Apply** adds the change to the recipe as one undo step, and **Cancel** drops it.

## Light

<PreviewCollection feature="lighting-colour" section="light" />

**Light** has **Exposure**, **Contrast**, **Highlights**, **Shadows**, **Whites**, and **Blacks**. Pick a control from the strip, then drag the ruler under it. Double-tap the value to reset that control; tap it to type an exact number.

## Colour

<PreviewCollection feature="lighting-colour" section="colour" />

**Colour** has three tabs:

- **Basics**: **Saturation** and **Vibrance**, plus **Protect skin tones**, which holds skin colours back while the rest of the photo changes.
- **HSL**: **Hue**, **Saturation**, and **Luminance** for eight colour bands (red, orange, yellow, green, aqua, blue, purple, and magenta). **Reset band** clears the selected band.
- **Wheels**: separate colour wheels for **Shadows**, **Midtones**, and **Highlights**. Drag around a wheel to pick the tint and toward its edge for more of it.

## Curves

<PreviewCollection feature="lighting-colour" section="curves" />

**Curves** edits the tone curve for **RGB** or one of the **Red**, **Green**, and **Blue** channels.

- Tap the curve to add a point and drag it; drag a point off the graph and release to remove it.
- The presets set the whole curve: **Auto**, **Linear**, **Soft contrast**, **Strong contrast**, **Fade**, and **Brighten**.
- **Reset channel** clears the current channel; **Reset curves** clears all four.

## White balance

<PreviewCollection feature="lighting-colour" section="white-balance" />

**White balance** sets **Temperature** and **Tint**, or applies a preset: **Auto**, **Daylight**, **Cloudy**, **Tungsten**, or **Fluorescent**. **Pick neutral** lets you tap something that should be grey; the editor balances the photo around it. Pick a mid grey rather than a bright white, since clipped whites carry no colour to measure.

## The histogram

The histogram button on the canvas opens a graph of the displayed preview with the share of clipped shadows and highlights. Tap the graph to cycle RGB, red, green, blue, and luminance. **Show clipping** in the editor's settings also marks clipped areas on the photo.

## Example: warm up a grey morning

1. Open the photo, tap **Tools**, then **White balance**.
2. Tap **Cloudy**, then nudge **Temperature** up a little more.
3. Open **Light** and lift **Shadows** until the foreground reads.
4. In **Colour › HSL**, pick the blue band and lower its **Saturation** so the sky stays calm.
