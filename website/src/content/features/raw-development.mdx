The ReFra editor opens camera RAW files (DNG, CR2, CR3, NEF, NRW, ARW, RAF, ORF, RW2, PEF, and SRW) and adds a **Develop** tool to the catalogue for them. Develop decides how the sensor data becomes a picture; every other tool then works on that result as usual.

## Develop

**Develop** has five tabs. The option tiles show a real develop of your photo, and every change lands in the recipe like any other tool.

- **White balance**: **Camera** (the setting recorded in the file), **Auto**, **Daylight**, or **Custom**, which unlocks **Temperature** and **Tint**.
- **Tone**: **Exposure**, **Brightness**, **Contrast**, **Shadows**, and **Highlights**.
- **Detail**: **Sharpen**, the **Demosaic** method (**Linear**, **VNG**, **PPG**, **AHD**, or **DCB**), **Noise reduction** (**Off**, **Low**, **Medium**, or **High**), and **Half resolution (faster)**, which develops at half size to save time.
- **Colour**: **Saturation**, **Vibrance**, and the **Colour space**: **sRGB**, **Adobe RGB**, **Wide**, or **ProPhoto**.
- **Output**: **Highlight recovery**, which treats blown highlights as **Clip**, **Blend**, or **Rebuild**.

**Reset develop** returns every tab to the neutral develop: camera white balance, sRGB, and AHD.

## Saving a developed copy

On a RAW file the header button reads **Export**. It opens **Save developed copy**, with **JPEG**, **PNG**, **TIFF (8-bit)**, and **TIFF (16-bit)**. TIFF keeps the full bit depth but only holds the develop itself: any other edit, such as a crop, a look, or markup, makes TIFF unavailable until you remove it.

To skip the question, set **RAW export** in the editor's settings to a fixed format. When that format is TIFF and the recipe has edits TIFF cannot hold, the editor asks again. See [saving copies](/ReFra/features/save-edits).

## Example: recover a bright sky

1. Open the RAW file and tap **Tools**, then **Develop**.
2. In **Output**, set **Highlight recovery** to **Blend**.
3. In **Tone**, lower **Highlights** and lift **Shadows**.
4. Tap **Export** and pick **TIFF (16-bit)** to keep the most detail for another app.
