Subject cutout isolates the thing you point at from its background, producing a transparent cutout you can copy, share, or save. It needs the **Subject Cutout** model group installed. See [AI models](/ReFra/features/ai-models).

## Two ways in

- **Viewer**: choose **Cut out subject** under **Long-press action** in [media viewer settings](/ReFra/features/viewer-settings), then touch and hold the subject in the open photo. The option needs the model downloaded first; until then it is disabled with a hint. A long press rotates by default, so choosing cutout replaces that gesture with an on-screen rotate button.
- **Editor**: in the [ReFra editor](/ReFra/features/editor), the **Retouch** group has **Cutout** and **Remove background**. Tap the photo to pick the subject, then switch between **Include** and **Exclude** and tap again to refine the selection; **Reset selection** starts over. **Cutout** can **Copy** or **Share** the subject as a transparent image. **Remove background** keeps the subject in the photo, and the saved copy is a transparent PNG. Both tools appear only when the model is installed.

## Refine the cutout

<PreviewCollection feature="subject-cutout" section="refine" />

1. After the first detection, tap **Include** and point at parts that were missed, or **Exclude** and point at parts to drop. Each point refines the mask.
2. **Undo**, **Redo**, and **Reset** step through or restart the refinement.
3. While it works, **Preparing** or **Refining cutout…** shows; **No object detected under long-press** means the point landed somewhere the model found nothing; aim at the subject and try again.

## Use the result

<PreviewCollection feature="subject-cutout" section="use-the-result" />

The transparent selection offers **Copy** (clipboard), **Share**, and **Save**; saving writes a PNG so the transparency survives. **Close cutout** exits the mode. In the viewer the controls float over the photo and the original file is untouched.

## Example: a sticker of the cat

1. Choose **Cut out subject** under **Long-press action**, open the cat photo, and hold on the cat.
2. Tap **Include** and touch the ear tip the mask missed.
3. **Save** to write the PNG, then share it anywhere a transparent image works.
