A RAW file in the [editor](/ReFra/features/legacy-editor) gets its own tab set: **Smart** (where available), **White balance**, **Tone**, **Detail**, **Colour**, **Output**, **Filters**, **Markup**, and **More**. Ordinary photos never show the RAW-only tabs, and RAW files do not show the regular Lighting/Effects tabs.

## White balance

<PreviewCollection feature="legacy-raw-development" section="white-balance" />

- **Camera**: use the white balance the camera recorded.
- **Auto**: the app estimates a correction.
- **Daylight**: a fixed daylight preset.
- **Custom**: reveals **Temperature** (2000–12000 K) and **Tint** sliders for manual control.

## Tone

<PreviewCollection feature="legacy-raw-development" section="tone" />

- **Exposure** (-3 to 3): brighten or darken the whole image in stops.
- **Brightness** (-2 to 2): overall lightness.
- **Contrast**, **Shadows**, and **Highlights** (-1 to 1 each): contrast strength, and how the darkest and brightest areas render.

## Detail

<PreviewCollection feature="legacy-raw-development" section="detail" />

- **Demosaic**: chooses how the RAW sensor data is turned into pixels; **Linear**, **VNG**, **PPG**, **AHD**, or **DCB**. They render the file differently; preview each on your photo.
- **Sharpen** (0 to 2): edge definition.
- **Noise Reduction**: **Off**, **Low**, **Medium**, **High**.
- **Half resolution**: renders and exports at reduced resolution for faster processing; leave it off for full detail.

## Colour and output

<PreviewCollection feature="legacy-raw-development" section="colour" />
<PreviewCollection feature="legacy-raw-development" section="output" />

- **Colour › Saturation** shifts every colour equally; **Vibrance** lifts muted colours more gently than already-strong ones.
- **Colour › Colour space**: **sRGB**, **Adobe RGB**, **Wide**, or **ProPhoto**. sRGB is the safe choice for sharing.
- **Output › Highlight recovery**: **Clip**, **Blend**, or **Rebuild** control how blown-out areas render; they can soften clipped highlights but cannot recover detail the sensor never captured.

## Saving a developed copy

<PreviewCollection feature="legacy-raw-development" section="saving" />

Tap **Save Copy** in the toolbar to open the **Save developed copy** sheet, which offers **JPEG**, **PNG**, **TIFF (8-bit)**, or **TIFF (16-bit)**. TIFF keeps full bit depth but cannot include your crop, filters, or markup; remove them to export TIFF. JPEG and PNG include everything you applied. The developed copy lands in `Pictures/Edited` with a `_developed` suffix, and the original RAW is never overwritten.

## Example: rescue a bright-window RAW

1. Open the RAW, tap **Edit**.
2. **White balance › Camera**, then **Tone › Highlights** down and **Shadows** up slightly.
3. **Colour › Colour space › sRGB**, and **Output › Highlight recovery › Rebuild** to soften the window.
4. Tap **Save Copy**, then choose **JPEG** in the sheet; a shareable photo appears in `Pictures/Edited` and the RAW stays untouched.
