A collection is a set of photos and videos you choose by hand, independent of folders. The same photo can sit in several collections without being duplicated: a collection stores references, not file copies, so it costs no extra storage.

Collections live at the bottom of the **Albums** tab, after the folders. Each shows a cover, its name, and how many items it holds.

## Create a collection

<PreviewCollection feature="collections" section="create-a-collection" />

Two ways in:

1. **From Library**: tap **+** beside the **Albums** heading, then type a name.
2. **From a selection**: select photos or touch and hold an album, tap **Add to collection**, and choose **New collection** in the sheet. Type a name and tap **Create and add**; the collection is created and the selection lands in it in one step.

## Add and remove items

- **Add media**: select photos on the Timeline, in an album, or inside another collection, then choose **Add to collection** and pick the target.
- **Add whole albums**: touch and hold a collection and choose **Add albums**, then pick the albums whose entire contents should join.
- **Remove items**: open the collection, select the items, and tap **Remove from collection**. The files stay in their folders and in every other collection; only the reference leaves.

## Manage a collection

<PreviewCollection feature="collections" section="manage-a-collection" />

Touch and hold a collection among the albums (the **Collections** filter chip shows only collections):

- **Pin / Unpin**: keep it in the pinned carousel.
- **Rename**: change its name.
- **Add albums**: bulk-add folders as above.
- **Delete collection**: removes the collection only. The confirmation spells it out: the media inside is not deleted.

Inside an open collection you get the normal media grid. Tap to view, touch and hold to select, and the usual share, copy, move, and delete actions work on the selected files themselves.

## Example: a holiday collection across folders

1. In **Albums**, open **Camera**, touch and hold the first holiday photo, and drag to select the run of holiday shots.
2. Tap **Add to collection**, choose **New collection**, type **Holiday 2026**, and tap **Create and add**.
3. Open **Screenshots**, select the boarding-pass screenshots, tap **Add to collection**, and pick **Holiday 2026**.
4. The collection now mixes Camera photos and Screenshots. Both folders are untouched, and removing an item from the collection leaves the file where it was.

## If a collection looks wrong

- Removing an item from a collection never deletes the file; check its original album.
- Deleting a collection is about the grouping, not the files. The confirmation says so before it happens.
- Collections are stored by the app itself, not as folders. Reinstalling or clearing the app's data removes them; the media files are unaffected.
