The photo editor can place other photos over the one you opened. Open **Layers** from the catalogue, or pin it to the toolbar; see [the ReFra editor](/ReFra/features/editor).

## The layer list

The list shows the stack top first. The photo you opened is the bottom row, marked **Base · Locked**; it cannot be moved or deleted. While the recipe has global adjustments, a **Light adjustment** row sits above it.

- **Add layer** opens the system picker for a JPEG or PNG photo. A photo can hold up to 16 image layers.
- Tap a row to select it. The eye icon shows or hides a layer.
- **Layer options** on a row offer **Rename**, **Duplicate**, **Move up**, **Move down**, **Lock** or **Unlock**, and **Delete**.

Every change to the list is one undo step.

## Double exposure

**Double exposure** (in the **Compose** group) is the quickest way to combine two photos. With no layer yet it offers **Add image**; after that it shows the base and overlay with a blend mode, **Opacity**, and shortcuts to transform the overlay or edit its mask.

## Transform

**Transform** moves the selected layer: **X** and **Y** position, **Scale**, and **Rotation**, with **Lock proportions**, **Align centre**, **Flip**, and **Reset transform**. While **Transform** is open you can also drag the layer and its corner handles on the canvas.

## Blend and opacity

**Blend & opacity** sets how the selected layer mixes with what is under it: **Normal**, **Multiply**, **Screen**, **Overlay**, or **Soft light**, plus **Opacity**. **Clip to layer below** limits the layer to the shape of the one beneath it. **View original**, in the header's **More** menu, shows the layer at **Normal** and full opacity until you turn it off.

## Masks

**Edit mask** on a layer opens the mask brush. **Erase** hides the layer where you brush and **Paint** brings it back; **Size**, **Hardness**, and **Flow** shape the brush. **Undo stroke** removes the last stroke and **Reset mask** clears the mask.

## Saving and recovery

A saved copy flattens every visible layer into one image; see [saving copies](/ReFra/features/save-edits). Layers point to their source photos, so if the editor restores an edit after Android stopped it and a layer's photo is gone, it shows **Missing media**: **Relink** picks a replacement, **Try again** retries, and **Discard saved edits** starts over.

## Example: a double exposure portrait

1. Open a portrait against a plain sky and tap **Tools**, then **Double exposure**.
2. Tap **Add image** and pick a forest photo.
3. Choose **Screen** and lower **Opacity** until both read.
4. Tap **Edit mask** and **Erase** the forest over the face.
