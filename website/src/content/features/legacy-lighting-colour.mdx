The **Lighting** and **Colour** tabs in the [editor](/ReFra/features/legacy-editor) hold the slider-based adjustments. Tap a tool, drag its slider, and the preview updates live; tapping back into another tool keeps your values.

## Lighting

<PreviewCollection feature="legacy-lighting-colour" section="lighting" />

- **Brightness**: overall lightness.
- **Tone**: the balance between dark and light areas.
- **Contrast**: separation between lights and darks.
- **Black point** / **White point**: where pure black and pure white start.
- **Highlights** / **Shadows**: brighten or darken the visible bright and dark ends separately.
- **Vignette**: darkens the corners.

## Colour

<PreviewCollection feature="legacy-lighting-colour" section="colour" />

- **Saturation**: how vivid all colours are.
- **Warmth**: cooler to warmer.
- **Tint**: the green-magenta balance.
- **Skin tone** and **Blue tone**: targeted shifts for those colour ranges.
- **Hue**: rotates every colour; its slider spans ±180 degrees.
- **B&W**: converts to black and white.

Most sliders run -100 to 100 around neutral; Vignette runs 0 to 100 and Hue uses degrees. **Undo** and **Redo** cover each change, and holding a tool you already applied removes that adjustment.

## Example: rescue a backlit portrait

1. Open the photo, tap **Edit**, and go to **Lighting**.
2. Raise **Shadows** until the face reads, then pull **Highlights** down so the window behind it stops blowing out.
3. In **Colour**, nudge **Warmth** up a little.
4. **Save Copy** so the unedited original stays beside it.
