**Settings › Smart Features** is the hub for everything the on-device AI powers: descriptive search, automatic categories, people grouping, subject cutout, and face blur. Model processing happens on your device. Two features deliberately hand data elsewhere when you use them: letting a connected cloud provider answer searches on its server, and [visual search](/ReFra/features/visual-search), which sends an image to another app. Sharing or casting media to other apps works as usual and is outside this scope.

## The hub rows

<PreviewCollection feature="smart-features" section="the-hub-rows" />

- **Include ignored albums**: whether media hidden by ignore rules still feeds Smart Features and Smart Search. Changing it starts a full refresh, and locked albums stay private either way. The switch is unavailable while a Smart Scan is running; wait for it or cancel it in the Smart Scan manager.
- **AI Models Manager**: download, install, or remove the model packs each feature needs. See [AI models](/ReFra/features/ai-models).
- **Categories**: opens the categories manager; enabled once the models are ready. See [categories](/ReFra/features/categories).
- **Cloud libraries**: appears once a cloud account is configured. **Server-side smart search** is one global switch that applies to connected servers with their own AI (currently Immich) and lets the server answer searches itself. Per-provider **Index on this device** switches; one per provider type, covering every account of that type, and make the app analyze that provider's media with the local AI, so even a provider with no AI of its own (a NAS share over SMB or WebDAV, for example) can join local search results. Leaving the switches off skips that provider's media in local scans.
- **Smart Features data**: the scan manager for refreshing and repairing the data Smart Features rely on. See [Smart Features data](/ReFra/features/smart-scan).
- **Edit Backups**: manages the original-image backups the editor keeps when you overwrite a photo, with their storage usage and cleanup controls.

## Privacy in one paragraph

Search indexes, categories, people groups, and cutouts are computed by the on-device models and stored locally. The **Cloud libraries** row is where you decide, per provider, whether remote media gets indexed on this device, answered by the server's own search, or left alone; server-side answers mean your search text goes to that server while it is connected.

## Example: search a NAS account alongside local photos

1. The user adds their NAS as a cloud account and wants its photos searchable like the local library, but the NAS has no AI of its own.
2. Under **Settings › Smart Features › AI Models**, they install a search engine (CLIP or EmbeddingGemma 2).
3. Under **Smart Features › Cloud libraries**, they turn on **Index on this device** for the NAS account.
4. They open **Smart Features data**, keep the **Entire library** preset, and let the scan finish; the NAS photos are analyzed on the phone and start appearing in search and people results.

## If a feature is missing

- Rows like **AI Models Manager** and **Categories** only appear on builds that include the AI feature; a build without it does not show them.
- **Categories** stays disabled until the required models are downloaded in the AI Models Manager.
- **Cloud libraries** needs a connected cloud account; with none, the row does not appear.
