The Library tab gathers everything that is not the plain timeline: shortcuts to special collections, smart sections, your cloud accounts, and all of your albums. Tap **Library** in the bottom bar.

## What is on the screen

<PreviewCollection feature="library" section="what-is-on-the-screen" />

- **Search bar**: at the top, same as the timeline.
- **Settings**: tap the ReFra mark in the shared header, or pick Settings from its menu sheet.
- **Shortcuts**: a grid of tiles that jump straight to a destination. Available tiles are **Trash**, **Favorites**, **Vault**, **Ignored**, **Memories**, **Private folder** (once configured), **Locations**, **People**, **Categories**, and, when a cloud account is connected, **Cloud archive**, **Shared links**, **Cloud backup & sync**, and **Cloud accounts**. Tiles only appear when their feature is available; a device that cannot use the system trash does not show Trash.
  - **Counts**: tiles that count items show the count right after their name, as in "Favorites · 12".
  - **Sizes**: on a phone row four units wide, a tile can be a 1×1 icon, a row 2, 3 or 4 units wide, or a larger card (2×2, 2×3 or 4×2). Tiles are placed in order and the grid does not go back to fill a gap, so a 3-unit tile shares its row only with a 1×1 that follows it.
  - **Cards**: as a card, Favorites shows recent favourites, and Trash shows its most recent items in grey with when the next one is deleted. Locations shows the place of your latest photo on a map, People shows faces, and Categories shows a few category thumbnails side by side. Vault shows blurred colour shapes instead of photos, locked or not, and Private folder shows no photos either. Vault shows how many vaults you have only when the vault list has no lock.
  - **Show more**: on a phone-width screen, tiles that do not fit in two rows (five while Locations, People or Categories is shown, or the height of the tallest card) fold behind a **Show more** tile. It turns into **Show less** and stays in the same spot, with the extra tiles laid out after it.

Locations appears when your photos carry location data, People when people grouping is set up, and Categories when the AI models are installed.

Below the shortcuts come your **albums** and collections, with their own filters, pinned carousel, and sections; see [albums](/ReFra/features/albums).

## Customize the shortcuts and sections

<PreviewCollection feature="library" section="customize-the-shortcuts-and-sections" />

1. Tap the **⋮** menu in the Library header and pick **Customize library**.
2. The **Customize library** editor opens. Touching and holding a tile or a section does not open it.
3. Now you can:
   - **Drag** a tile to reorder it.
   - **Tap a tile** to select it, then **drag its side handle** to snap between 1, 2, 3 and 4 units wide, or **drag its bottom handle** to make it taller. Tapping a handle steps to the next size. The handles work at the screen edge too; dragging one there does not trigger the back gesture.
   - With a Favorites tile selected, pick **Latest**, **Oldest** or **Shuffle** to choose which favourites it shows. Shuffle picks again each time you open the Library. On a card, **Layout** switches between **Grid** and **Scrapbook**, which lays the photos over each other like prints.
   - **Tap the ×** on a tile to remove it; it moves to a tray below. Locations, People and Categories are hidden and brought back the same way.
   - **Tap a tile in the tray** to bring a hidden shortcut back.
4. Below the tiles, a **Sections** card has switches for **All shortcuts** (show every tile instead of folding them behind **Show more**), **Pinned albums**, **Album sections**, and **Media types**.
5. **Albums order** at the bottom opens the album arrange screen; see [albums](/ReFra/features/albums). It explains why when the albums cannot be arranged.
6. Tap **Done** to keep the layout.

## Example: a minimal library

1. Open **Library**, tap **⋮**, and pick **Customize library**.
2. Tap the × on **Ignored** and **Cloud accounts**; they move to the tray marked hidden.
3. Tap the × on **Categories** if you do not use AI grouping.
4. Drag **Favorites** to the first position and tap **Done**.
5. The Library now opens straight to Favorites plus the tiles you kept.

## If something is missing

- **A tile you expect is absent.** The feature behind it may be off or unavailable on this device: Trash requires the system trash, the cloud tiles need a connected account, and Locations, People and Categories need something to show. Check the hidden tray in **⋮ › Customize library** first; hidden tiles can be re-added from there, even when every tile is hidden.
- **People or Categories are empty.** They need the AI models; see **Settings › Smart Features**.
- **Locations shows nothing.** Media needs location data, and the optional media-location permission must be granted. See [first launch](/ReFra/features/first-launch) for that permission.
