The Library tab gathers everything that is not the plain timeline: shortcuts to special collections, smart sections, your cloud accounts, and all of your albums. Tap **Library** in the bottom bar.

## What is on the screen

<PreviewCollection feature="library" section="what-is-on-the-screen" />

- **Search bar**: at the top, same as the timeline.
- **Settings**: tap the ReFra mark in the shared header, or pick Settings from its menu sheet.
- **Shortcuts**: a grid of tiles that jump straight to a destination. Available tiles are **Trash**, **Favorites**, **Vault**, **Ignored**, **Private folder** (once configured), and, when a cloud account is connected, **Cloud archive**, **Shared links**, **Cloud backup & sync**, and **Cloud accounts**. Tiles only appear when their feature is available; a device that cannot use the system trash does not show Trash. Tiles that count items, like Trash and Favorites, show the current count. On a phone-width screen with more than four tiles, the grid shows the first three plus a **Show more** tile; tap it to reveal the rest. It turns into **Show less** and stays in the same spot, with the extra tiles laid out after it.
- **Locations**: a map preview and rows of places, when your photos carry location data.
- **People**: recognized faces, when people grouping is set up.
- **Categories**: AI-sorted groups such as nature or food, when the AI models are installed; otherwise the section explains how to turn them on.

Below the sections come your **albums** and collections, with their own filters, pinned carousel, and sections; see [albums](/ReFra/features/albums).

Empty sections hide themselves rather than showing blank boxes, so the Library only displays what has content.

## Customize the shortcuts and sections

<PreviewCollection feature="library" section="customize-the-shortcuts-and-sections" />

1. Tap the **⋮** menu in the Library header and pick **Customize library**.
2. The **Customize library** editor opens. Touching and holding a tile or a section does not open it.
3. Now you can:
   - **Drag** a tile to reorder it.
   - **Drag the side handle** on a tile to switch between half and full width.
   - **Tap the ×** on a tile to remove it; it moves to a tray below.
   - **Tap a tile in the tray** to bring a hidden shortcut back.
4. In the same edit mode, a **Sections** card appears with a switch per section to show or hide it: **Locations**, **People**, **Categories**, **Pinned albums**, **Album sections**, and **Media types**.
5. Tap **Done** to keep the layout.

The same section switches also live under **Settings › Timeline & Albums**, so a hidden section can always be brought back from settings too.

## Example: a minimal library

1. Open **Library**, tap **⋮**, and pick **Customize library**.
2. Tap the × on **Ignored** and **Cloud accounts**; they move to the tray marked hidden.
3. In the **Sections** card, switch off **Categories** if you do not use AI grouping.
4. Drag **Favorites** to the first position and tap **Done**.
5. The Library now opens straight to Favorites plus the sections you kept.

## If something is missing

- **A tile you expect is absent.** The feature behind it may be off or unavailable on this device: Trash requires the system trash, and the cloud tiles need a connected account. Check the hidden tray in **⋮ › Customize library** first; hidden tiles can be re-added from there, even when every tile is hidden.
- **People or Categories are empty.** They need the AI models; see **Settings › Smart Features**.
- **Locations shows nothing.** Media needs location data, and the optional media-location permission must be granted. See [first launch](/ReFra/features/first-launch) for that permission.
