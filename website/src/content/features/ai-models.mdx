**Settings › Smart Features › Intelligence Hub** is where the machine-learning models live. They are separate downloads, each one powering a feature, and the features run on your device: the hub states that your photos, faces, and searches never leave it.

## The hub

<PreviewCollection feature="ai-models" section="the-hub" />

The summary at the top counts how many models are ready and how much space they use. Below it, **Features** lists three entries, each with its status, such as **Not downloaded** with the download size, **Downloading** with a percentage, or **Ready** with the size on disk:

| Feature | Download | What it unlocks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Smart search** | ~150–320 MB, by engine | Descriptive text search, visual similarity search, and automatic [categories](/ReFra/features/categories). |
| **Face grouping** | ~131 MB | Grouping the same person across photos in [People](/ReFra/features/people), and the editor's **Blur faces** and **Mosaic faces** markup actions. |
| **Subject cutout** | ~44 MB | [Subject cutout](/ReFra/features/subject-cutout) in the viewer and the editor's **Cutout** and **Remove background** tools. |

Tap a feature to open its page: what it does, a preview, its options, and one button that downloads the models and turns the feature on, such as **Download & turn on search**. Once installed, the same button removes the models after a confirmation.

## Choosing a search engine

Smart search and categories run on one of two engines, picked under **Search engine** on the Smart search page. Switching engines indexes your photos again. **Compare engines** opens a side-by-side sheet covering accuracy, speed, languages, and video support.

| | CLIP | EmbeddingGemma 2 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Embedding size | 512 | 768 |
| Understands | Text &amp; images | Text, images &amp; videos |
| Download size | ~150 MB | ~320 MB |
| Indexing speed | Fast | Slower (larger model) |
| Result quality | Good | Higher |
| Languages | Mostly English | 100+ languages |

CLIP is the default and the lighter option; EmbeddingGemma 2 is more accurate, multilingual, and can index video content as well as images. Removing the engine in use stops search until another engine is installed and selected.

## Face grouping quality

The Face grouping page has a **Quality** choice that decides how faces are found and compared. **Standard** (UltraFace and ArcFace) is the one available now; **Fast** and **Accurate** are listed as **Coming soon**. Changing the quality scans your photos again, because each tier compares faces in its own way.

## Scan options

The feature pages for Smart search and Face grouping share two options:

- **Scan only while charging**: background scans for People and search wait for the charger.
- **Skip hidden albums**: hidden albums stay out of People and search. Locked albums are never scanned.

## Bundled and offline builds

- On a build that bundles the models, the button reads **Install** and restores the bundled copies with no download; the status reads **Preparing…** while they are set up.
- On an offline build without bundled models, missing models cannot be downloaded, and the feature says so. A feature the build does not include shows **Not available in this build**.

After installing new models, run a refresh under **Smart Features data** so existing media gets indexed; see [Smart Features data](/ReFra/features/smart-scan).

## Example: people grouping without the big search pack

1. Open **Settings › Smart Features › Intelligence Hub**.
2. Tap **Face grouping**, then **Download & start grouping**.
3. Leave **Smart search** off if descriptive search is not needed.
4. Open **Smart Features data** and refresh persons; the Library's People section starts filling in.

## If a download fails or is absent

- Retry the download on a stable connection; Wi-Fi is recommended. Each feature shows its download size before you start, so you can check space first.
- On an offline build, only bundled models can be installed; there is no download path, so a missing model stays unavailable on that build.
