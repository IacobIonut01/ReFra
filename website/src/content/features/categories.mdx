Categories group photos by what they contain rather than where they are stored. Classification runs on your device using the downloaded AI models; the settings screen states that no media is uploaded for it. Without the models installed, categories do not appear.

## Browse categories

<PreviewCollection feature="categories" section="browse-categories" />

1. Open **Library › Categories**. The summary at the top shows how many photos are sorted, or the progress while sorting runs, with **Pause**.
2. The filters narrow the list: **All**, **Pinned**, **Made by you**, and **Suggested**. Pinned categories sit at the top; the rest are under **Other categories**.
3. Tap a category to open its grid, which behaves like any album view. A tile that reads **Sorting…** is still filling in; **No matches yet** means nothing has matched so far.

Categories need the Smart search model. Until it is installed, the screen shows **Turn on Smart search first** with **Set up Smart search**; see [AI models](/ReFra/features/ai-models).

## Create a category

<PreviewCollection feature="categories" section="create-a-category" />

Tap **Create a category**, or start from one of the suggestions under **Or start with a suggestion** (**Add all suggestions** adds every one). Creating takes three steps:

1. **Describe**: give it a **name**, for example "Receipts", and under **Describe what to include** write a plain sentence such as "photos of my dog at the beach". **Quick start** suggestions fill in both fields. A count of photos that already look like it updates as you type.
2. **Examples** (optional): pick a few photos that clearly show what you mean. Close-ups where the subject fills the frame work best.
3. **Strictness**: **Broad** includes loosely related photos, **Balanced** is a good mix, **Strict** keeps only strong matches, and **Exact** keeps near-perfect matches only. **Fine-tune** slides between more results and more accuracy; it is a matching preference, not a confidence guarantee. **See all matches** shows the full list before you save.

The category is saved and sorting starts; its photos fill in as photos are checked.

## Edit, pin, or delete a category

Open a category for its options:

- **Pin** or **Unpin**: show it at the top of Categories, or move it back to **Other categories**.
- **Edit**: change the name, description, example photos, and strictness. When nothing matches, **Loosen strictness** and **Add example photos** are offered directly.
- **Change cover**: pick one of the category's photos for its tile, or **Use best match** to go back to the automatic cover.
- **Delete**: removes the category and its settings. The photos stay in your library and in other categories.

## Categories settings

<PreviewCollection feature="categories" section="categories-settings" />

Under **Categories Settings**:

- **Scan for new categories**: runs a scan over the library, showing "Scanning media…" while it works.
- **Stop scan**: ends the current scan while keeping completed results.
- **Exclude videos**: restricts matching to photos (categories can otherwise include videos, which the index embeds from sampled frames). Turning it on asks whether to remove video matches that already exist.
- **Reset categories**: deletes all categories and reinitializes the defaults. This is destructive to custom categories; it does not touch photos.

If the models are missing, the page says so and points at **Settings › Smart Features** to download them.

## Example: a receipts category for tax season

1. Open **Library › Categories** and tap **Create a category**; name it **Receipts**.
2. Describe it as "photos of receipts, invoices, and order confirmations".
3. Add two or three real receipts as example photos.
4. Choose **Balanced**, check **See all matches**, and save.
5. New receipts you photograph are sorted into it as they are checked.

## If matches look wrong

- Too much noise: raise the strictness toward **Strict** or add more example photos.
- Too few matches: drop toward **Broad**, or loosen the description.
- A category with nothing in it does not prove the model is missing; it can also mean no match above the strictness bar or sorting still in progress. Check model and scan status under **Settings › Smart Features**.
