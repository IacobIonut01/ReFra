Categories group photos by what they contain rather than where they are stored. Classification runs on your device using the downloaded AI models; the settings screen states that no media is uploaded for it. Without the models installed, categories do not appear.

## Browse categories

1. Open **Library**. The **Categories** section shows a row of cards with a match count each.
2. Tap a category to open its grid, which behaves like any album view.
3. An empty section means either no models are installed or no scan has matched anything yet.

## Create a category

1. In the Categories section, tap **Add category**.
2. Give it a **name**, for example "Receipts" or "Dog".
3. Under **Describe what to include**, write a plain sentence describing the photos it should contain, such as "photos of my dog at the beach". Or tap a **Quick start** suggestion to fill this in.
4. Set the **Sensitivity**: **Broad** includes loosely related photos, **Balanced** is the default mix, **Strict** keeps only strong matches, and **Exact** keeps near-perfect matches only. A **Fine-tune** slider adjusts within the choice, trading more results against more accuracy; it is a matching preference, not a confidence guarantee.
5. Optionally add **Reference photos**: tap **Add photos** and pick examples so the category can match visually similar ones.
6. The **Preview** shows the best matches; tap **See all matches** for the full list before committing.
7. Tap **Create**. The category is saved and classification starts; its photos fill in as the scan runs.

## Edit or delete a category

- Open the category and tap **Edit category** to change the name, description, sensitivity, or reference photos, then **Save**. Saving re-runs classification for the category.
- **Delete category** removes the grouping and asks for confirmation that the action cannot be undone. Deleting the group leaves the photos themselves in the library.

## Categories settings

Under **Categories Settings**:

- **Scan for new categories**: runs a scan over the library, showing "Scanning media…" while it works.
- **Stop scan**: ends the current scan while keeping completed results.
- **Exclude videos**: restricts matching to photos (categories can otherwise include videos, which the index embeds from sampled frames). Turning it on asks whether to remove video matches that already exist.
- **Reset categories**: deletes all categories and reinitializes the defaults. This is destructive to custom categories; it does not touch photos.

If the models are missing, the page says so and points at **Settings › Smart Features** to download them.

## Example: a receipts category for tax season

1. **Library › Categories › Add category**, name it **Receipts**.
2. Describe it as "photos of receipts, invoices, and order confirmations".
3. Add two or three real receipts as **Reference photos**.
4. Set sensitivity to **Balanced**, check the preview, and tap **Create**.
5. New receipts you photograph get classified into it on the next scan.

## If matches look wrong

- Too much noise: raise the sensitivity toward **Strict** or add more reference photos.
- Too few matches: drop toward **Broad**, or loosen the description.
- A scan that produced nothing at all does not prove the models are missing; it can also mean no match above the sensitivity bar or a scan still in progress. Check model and scan status under **Settings › Smart Features**.
