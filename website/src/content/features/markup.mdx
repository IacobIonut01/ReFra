**Markup** is pinned to the toolbar by default and sits in the **Retouch** group of the catalogue. Strokes and text stay editable while the tool is open; **Apply** adds the whole markup session to the recipe as one undo step.

## Brushes and modes

<PreviewCollection feature="markup" section="brushes-and-modes" />

The tabs pick the brush: **Pen**, **Highlighter**, **Blur**, **Mosaic**, and **Text**.

- **Pen** and **Highlighter** draw in the colour you pick from the swatch row. **Size** sets the stroke width.
- **Blur** and **Mosaic** obscure what you paint over. They have **Size** and **Strength**.

The buttons under the header switch what a finger does on the photo: **Draw**, **Move and zoom** (to work on a detail), or **Erase**, which removes a whole stroke when you touch it. **Clear markup** removes every stroke and text label.

## Text

1. Choose **Text**, then **Add text** and type.
2. Drag a label to move it; tap it again to edit the words.
3. With a label selected, pick a colour from the swatch row, or tap **Remove** to delete it.
4. Tap **Done** to finish typing.

## Blur or mosaic faces

When the **Face Detection** model is installed (see [AI models](/ReFra/features/ai-models)), **Blur** gets a **Blur faces** action and **Mosaic** gets **Mosaic faces**. It finds the faces in the photo and covers each with a stroke of the active brush. Those strokes erase and undo like any other, so you can touch up the result by hand.

Automatic face detection can miss a face. Zoom in and check the result before you share it.

## Example: hide an address label

1. Open the photo and tap **Markup**.
2. Pick **Mosaic**, raise **Size**, and paint over the label.
3. Switch to **Move and zoom**, zoom in, and check the edges; switch back to **Draw** for any gaps.
4. Tap **Apply**, then save a copy.
