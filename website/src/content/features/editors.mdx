**Settings › Editors** picks which app the edit actions open for photos and videos, and how edited images are re-encoded when they are saved. The same page is one tap away from the **Editors** row in [media viewer settings](/ReFra/features/viewer-settings).

## Image editor

<PreviewCollection feature="editors" section="image-editor" />

The **Image editor** tiles choose the app that opens when you edit a photo. The whole tile is the control; the selected app shows a filled radio marker.

- **ReFra editor (preview)**: the default. Opens the [ReFra editor](/ReFra/features/editor) for the formats it supports; anything else falls back to the built-in editor.
- **Built-in editor**: the classic editor with crop, rotate, filters, markup, and [RAW development](/ReFra/features/legacy-raw-development).
- Every compatible photo editor installed on the device appears as its own tile, named after the app.

## Video editor

The **Video editor** tiles choose the app that opens when you edit a video.

- **ReFra editor**: the default; see [video editor](/ReFra/features/video-editor).
- **Ask every time**: the system's app chooser opens on each edit instead of a fixed default.
- Installed video editors appear as their own tiles.

## Save quality

**Save quality** controls how edited and rotated images are re-encoded when they are saved; see [save edits](/ReFra/features/save-edits) for what a save preserves.

- **Manual** off (Auto): saves lossless where the format supports it (JXL) and at high quality otherwise.
- **Manual** on: adds two sliders — **Lossy quality** (1 to 100) and **JXL effort** (1 to 9).
