## Filters

<PreviewCollection feature="legacy-filters-effects" section="filters" />

The **Filters** tab shows the photo with each look applied as thumbnails. The full list:

Original, Lite, Playa, Honey, Isla, Desert, Clay, Palma, Blush, Alpaca, Modena, West, Metro, Reel, Bazaar, Ollie, Onyx, Eiffel, Vogue, Vista, Astro, Negative.

- Tap a filter to preview it full size; **Original** removes the filter.
- An **Intensity** slider (0 to 100) controls how strongly the chosen filter applies, so a look can be a hint instead of a blanket.
- The names are the label; compare them on your own photo rather than guessing from the word.

## Effects

<PreviewCollection feature="legacy-filters-effects" section="effects" />

The **Effects** tab holds three adjustable effects:

- **Posterize**: reduces the image to fewer colour bands for a graphic look.
- **Edges**: draws the picture's edges, sketch-style.
- **Borders**: adds a frame around the image; its strength is adjustable.

Each has its own control under the preview; **Undo** covers every change and a held applied tool removes it, same as the other tabs.

## Example: a softer look for a portrait

1. Open the photo, tap **Edit › Filters**.
2. Try a few (Blush, Palma) on the live preview.
3. Settle on one, then drag **Intensity** down to about 40 so it stays subtle.
4. **Save Copy**; the unfiltered original is unaffected.
