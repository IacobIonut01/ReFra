**Settings › Navigation** controls where the app starts, how the navigation bar behaves, and what a media selection offers. Every option is on this page except the per-action details, which live one level deeper under **Selection Actions**.

## Options

<PreviewCollection feature="navigation" section="options" />

- **Set default launch screen**: which tab opens when you start ReFra. **Auto** (marked with an "A") returns to the screen you were on when the app closed. Choosing **Timeline** or **Library** always starts there instead.
- **Use Material Navigation**: switches to the classic Material navigation bar with text labels, with a **Search** item between **Timeline** and **Library**. On larger screens and windows it becomes a side rail rather than a bottom bar.
- **Open keyboard on search**: the keyboard opens by itself when you enter search. Turn it off to keep the suggestions visible until you tap the field.
- **Hide navigation bar on scroll**: the bottom bar slides away while scrolling. The matching switch for the top bar, **Hide on scroll**, is under **Settings › Timeline & albums › Top bar**.
- **Display Selection Item's Title**: shows a text label under each action in the selection sheet (Share, Move, and the rest). Turn it off for a compact icon-only sheet.
- **Selection Actions**: opens the editor for which actions the selection sheet offers and in what order. See [selection actions](/ReFra/features/selection-actions).

## Example: always start in Library

1. Open **Settings › Navigation**.
2. Tap **Set default launch screen** and choose **Library**.
3. Close and reopen ReFra. It starts on Library, with your shortcuts and albums, instead of resuming where you left off.
4. To return to resume behavior, set it back to **Auto**.

## If the bars behave oddly

- A bar that vanishes while scrolling is one of the two auto-hide switches; scroll up to bring it back, or turn the switch off. The bottom bar's is here; the top bar's is **Hide on scroll** under **Settings › Timeline & albums › Top bar**.
- Selection labels are controlled separately from the navigation bar; an icon-only selection sheet means **Display Selection Item's Title** is off.
