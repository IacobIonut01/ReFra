**Crop & straighten** handles cropping, rotation, flips, straightening, and keystone correction in one tool. It is pinned to the toolbar as **Crop**. **Perspective** in the catalogue opens the same tool on its **Vertical** ruler.

## Crop

<PreviewCollection feature="crop-rotate" section="crop" />

- Drag the frame's edges or corners to crop; drag inside the frame to move the photo under it. The label above the frame shows the ratio and the output size in pixels.
- The ratio menu offers **Free**, **Original**, **1:1**, **4:5**, **3:2**, **4:3**, and **16:9**. The button next to it switches the ratio between portrait and landscape.
- **Rotate left** turns the photo 90 degrees; **Flip** mirrors it horizontally.
- **Apply Crop** keeps the result; **Reset** returns the frame to the full photo.

The crop always stays inside the photo, so straightening or keystone correction never leaves blank corners.

## Straighten and keystone

<PreviewCollection feature="crop-rotate" section="straighten-and-keystone" />

The menu on the right picks what the ruler changes:

- **Straighten** tilts the photo in fine steps to level a horizon.
- **Vertical** and **Horizontal** correct converging lines, such as a building shot from below.

Double-tap the value above the ruler to reset it to zero, or tap it to type an exact number. **Show grid** in the editor's settings draws a grid while you straighten.

## Guided upright

Drag the controls up to show **Flip vertical** and **Guided upright**. With **Guided upright** on, drag along up to four lines in the photo that should be straight; the editor solves the correction as you draw. **Clear guides** removes them.

## Example: level a skyline

1. Open the photo and tap **Crop**.
2. Pick **Vertical** and drag the ruler until the building edges stand upright.
3. Switch to **Straighten** and level the horizon.
4. Choose **16:9**, adjust the frame, and tap **Apply Crop**.
