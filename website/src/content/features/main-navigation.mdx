ReFra has two top-level destinations, Timeline and Library, reached from the navigation bar at the bottom of the screen. Everything else hangs off one of them.

## The navigation bar

<PreviewCollection feature="main-navigation" section="the-navigation-bar" />

The default floating dock holds the two destinations, with a separate Search button beside them:

- **Timeline**: all photos and videos, newest first.
- **Library**: shortcuts such as Favorites, Trash, and Vault; People, Locations, and Categories; and your albums and collections. See [Library](/ReFra/features/library) and [albums](/ReFra/features/albums).

Each tab keeps its own scroll position and open folder. Switching to another tab and back returns you to exactly where you were, including an album you had open.

On larger screens and windows, the destinations move into a side rail. When a timeline photo is open in a wide window, the gallery and viewer sit side by side; close the photo to return to the rail.

## Search

Tap the Search button beside the floating dock. The keyboard opens automatically once the search screen settles. Type part of a file name for text search, or a description like "sunset at the beach" when the AI models are installed. Recent searches are kept for quick re-use. If you would rather browse suggestions before typing, turn off **Settings › Navigation › Open keyboard on search**. See [search](/ReFra/features/search) for the full guide.

The dock hides while you are selecting items, so a Search button appears at the top, next to the filter icon. Your selection carries over to the search results.

The filter icon in the Timeline's top-right control group opens the [timeline filter sheet](/ReFra/features/timeline-filters), which narrows the grid by type, favorites, year, or album.

## Settings and back behavior

- **Settings**: Timeline and Library share the same compact header. Tap the ReFra mark or pick Settings from the header's menu sheet.
- **What's new**: the header's menu sheet lists it only while a release is unread; the dot on the menu button marks that unread version without covering the photo grid.
- **Back**: the system back gesture or button closes the current thing first: an open photo, a selection, an album, then the tab, then the app.
- **Selection**: touch and hold any thumbnail to start selecting; tap others to add them, or keep your finger down and drag to sweep over a run of items. Tap **X** or press back to cancel; clearing every pick with **Remove All** keeps the selection open. The full action list is on [manage media](/ReFra/features/manage-media).

## Making the bar yours

<PreviewCollection feature="main-navigation" section="making-the-bar-yours" />

Under **Settings › Navigation** you can change how moving around works:

- **Set default launch screen**: choose which screen opens on launch: Auto (the last screen you used), Timeline, or Library.
- **Use Material Navigation**: switch between the floating icon dock and the classic Material bar with text labels. Both styles use a side rail on larger screens.
- **Hide navigation bar on scroll**: let the bottom bar slide away as you scroll down and return when you scroll up. The top bar has its own switch, **Hide on scroll**, under **Settings › Timeline & albums › Top bar**.
- **Selection actions**: reorder or hide the actions shown when you select items, and show or hide their text labels.

## Example: jump to an old month

1. On the **Timeline**, drag the scrollbar handle at the right edge downward.
2. The grid jumps through months quickly; release when the header shows the month you want.
3. Tap a photo, swipe through a few, then press back. You return to the same scroll position.
4. Tap **Albums**, open a folder, then tap **Timeline** again. Your position is still there.

## If the bar is missing

The navigation bar can hide itself while you scroll if **Hide navigation bar on scroll** is enabled; scroll up a little and it slides back. On very small screens, **Use Material Navigation** under **Settings › Navigation** switches to a more compact bar.
