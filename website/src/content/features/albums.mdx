Albums group your media by where it lives: each device folder that contains photos or videos (Camera, Screenshots, Downloads, WhatsApp, and so on) and each album on a connected cloud account. They live on the **Library** screen, below its shortcuts and sections; see [Library](/ReFra/features/library).

## Find the albums

Tap **Library** in the navigation bar and scroll past the shortcuts. The albums part shows, top to bottom:

1. **Albums** with the number of albums, the sort and view controls, and **+** to start a [collection](/ReFra/features/collections).
2. **Filter chips**: **All**, **On device**, a chip per album section, **Collections**, **Cloud** when a cloud account is connected, and **Media types**.
3. **Pinned albums**: a carousel of the albums you pinned; it only appears once you pin something.
4. **Album sections**: collapsible groups such as Common and Apps, then **Others** for everything else.
5. **Media types**: virtual albums that collect all videos, photos, GIFs, or RAW files across folders.

Pinned albums, album sections, and media types can each be switched off while customizing the Library. Cloud albums carry the provider's icon, and albums others shared with you carry a **Shared** badge.

An album with a small lock icon is locked: opening it asks for your fingerprint or PIN first. See [locked albums](/ReFra/features/hidden-content).

## Browse and find an album

<PreviewCollection feature="albums" section="browse-and-find-an-album" />

- **Grid or list**: the view toggle beside the sort control switches between a thumbnail grid and a compact list. Pinch the grid to change its column count.
- **Sort**: the sort control orders the albums.
- **Arrange albums**: open it from the header menu, from **Rearrange** in an album's or collection's touch-and-hold menu, or from **Albums order** in **Customize library**. Touch and hold an album and drag it into place. **Reset** returns to the automatic order and **Done** keeps yours. New albums appear under **Others**. Arranging needs at least two albums and **Album sections** turned off.
- **Inside an album**: tap an album to open it; its media appears in a date-grouped grid with the same gestures as the timeline. Pinch to resize, touch and hold to select, tap the check circle on a date header to select that group.

## Album actions

<PreviewCollection feature="albums" section="album-actions" />

Touch and hold any album to open its actions:

- **Pin / Unpin**: keep the album in the pinned carousel.
- **Change album cover**: pick any photo as the cover instead of the most recent one.
- **Add to group**: bundle albums under a named, collapsible group.
- **Move to section**: override which collapsible section the album sits in.
- **Merge subfolders**: fold a folder's subfolders into a single album view; **Unmerge subfolders** splits them back.
- **Lock album / Unlock album**: require authentication to open it.
- **Add to ignored albums**: start an ignore rule for the folder; the follow-up steps ask whether it hides from the Timeline, Library, or both, without deleting files. See [ignored albums](/ReFra/features/ignored-albums).
- **Move album to trash / Delete album permanently**: removes the album's contents; the permanent option cannot be undone.

Groups, sections, covers, and merging are covered step by step in [album organization](/ReFra/features/album-organization).

## Example: find the Screenshots folder fast

1. Open **Library** and scroll to the albums.
2. Touch and hold **Screenshots**, then tap **Pin**.
3. Screenshots now sits in the pinned carousel above the other albums, whatever the sort order.
4. Tap **On device** to hide cloud albums and collections while you look.

## If an album is missing

- Its folder may have no media, or it may be covered by an ignore rule for albums. Check **Library › Ignored**.
- A filter chip other than **All** may be selected.
- The same folder name on two storage locations can appear as one album if **Merge albums by name** is on under **Settings › Timeline & Albums**.
- New folders appear after Android indexes them; pull down to refresh.
