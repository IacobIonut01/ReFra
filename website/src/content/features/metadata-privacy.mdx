**Remove metadata** on the details panel writes a cleaned file with the metadata you pick stripped out. The sheet opens with a one-line summary of what the file carries, for example "JPEG · Found location, dates & times, camera settings", and two presets:

- **Remove all**: everything that isn't needed to show the media.
- **Location only**: coordinates, place names and direction. It is disabled when the file has no location data.

Supported formats are **JPEG, PNG, WebP, GIF, JPEG 2000, JPEG XL, HEIF/HEIC, AVIF, MP4, MOV and 3GP**. TIFF and RAW files, PSD, SVG, BMP and other video containers such as MKV and WebM are not cleaned; the sheet shows the reason and the action stays disabled.

## Choose what to remove

<PreviewCollection feature="metadata-privacy" section="choose-what-to-remove" />

**Choose what to remove** expands into one checkbox per category found in the file, each with a preview of its value: Location, Dates & times, Device & owner, Captions & authorship, Camera settings, People, Edit history, Content credentials and Embedded thumbnail. Categories the file doesn't contain are not listed.

Pixels, orientation, colour profiles, HDR gain maps and Motion Photo video always stay intact. Removal does not touch faces visible in the picture or the file name; rename or crop the file separately for a fuller privacy pass.

Some videos record GPS in a telemetry track that is part of the media itself. On those, Location can't be removed and the sheet says so.

## Save a copy or replace

Pick the outcome under the presets. ReFra re-reads the cleaned file before anything is written: the selected metadata must be gone, the metadata you kept must still be there, and the image or video data must match the original exactly. If any check fails, nothing is saved.

- **Save copy**: writes the cleaned copy next to the original and leaves the original unchanged.
- **Replace original**: writes the cleaned file, then moves the original to system Trash. System Trash needs Android 11 or later; on older versions Replace original is disabled.

## Example: a beach photo without the GPS pin

1. Open the photo and go to **Media Info › Remove metadata**.
2. Tap **Location only**.
3. Keep **Save copy**, tap the button, then open the copy's details to confirm the Location row is gone before sharing it.
