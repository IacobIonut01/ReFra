**Settings › Timeline & Albums** controls how media is laid out, grouped, and labeled across the app. The page has three blocks: timeline behavior, album behavior, and per-screen display options.

## Timeline

<PreviewCollection feature="timeline-albums" section="timeline" />

- **Timeline layout**: **Grid** shows equal-sized thumbnails; **Mosaic** varies tile sizes for a denser, magazine-like look. Pinch the grid to change its density; the chosen range differs per layout.
- **Timeline order**: the date that places and groups items. **Capture time** uses the date embedded in the photo or video, falling back to the file's modified date when none exists. **Date modified** uses when the file last changed. Each choice offers **Newest first** and **Oldest first**.
- **Group similar photos**: collapses related items into one tile so the grid stays tidy; grouped items open together in the viewer. Four switches control what counts as similar:
  - **RAW+JPG pairs**: a RAW file and its processed counterpart sharing name and folder.
  - **Edited copies**: files with copy or edit suffixes such as `(1)`, `_edited`, or `.NIGHT`.
  - **Burst sequences**: burst shots that share a timestamp.
  - **Cloud + local copies**: the same file present on the device and on a cloud account; the tile gets a cloud badge.
- **Animate GIFs in grid**: plays GIF animations in the timeline and album grids. Turn it off if folders full of GIFs feel laggy.
- **Monthly highlights**: opens each month with a card of its best photos, how many photos and videos it holds, and where they were taken. Highlights are picked on the device: favorites first, then the largest photos, spread across different days. Months with fewer than 12 items keep the plain header. Touch and hold a card to select the whole month. **Play month recaps** adds a play button that starts a slideshow of the month.
- **Show places**: day headers and month cards name the city where most of their photos were taken; tap the place to see every photo taken there.
- **Floating date label**: keeps the date you are in floating at the top while you scroll. Tap it to see every month and jump to one. Turned off, the date shows as a plain sticky header.
- **Recheck capture dates**: re-reads the embedded dates of visible local media. Useful after copying files whose modified dates no longer reflect when they were taken.
- **Top bar**: one page for the bar at the top of Timeline, with a live preview that redraws as you change it. **Browsing** and **Selecting** under the preview switch only the picture, to show the bar during a selection. Its summary lists the buttons that are on.
  - **Select**: a check circle that starts selecting without a long press; tap photos to pick them. During a selection it becomes **X**, which ends it.
  - **Sort and filter**: the filter icon next to the menu. Turn it off if you never filter.
  - **Shortcut**: the button next to Select. Choose **Favorites** (the default), **Trash**, **Vault**, **Camera**, or **None**. Favorites and Trash are offered only on devices that support them, and Camera only when a camera app is installed. If you had turned off the old Favorites switch, the shortcut starts as None.
  - **Hide on scroll**: slides the top bar away while you scroll down, on Timeline and Library.
- **Story cards**: opens the story card settings; see [memories](/ReFra/features/memories).
- **Date formats**: opens the per-context date pattern editor; see [date formats](/ReFra/features/date-formats).

## Albums

- **Merge albums by name**: shows same-named albums from different storage locations as one album; the folders stay separate on disk.
- **Update modified date**: moves copied, renamed, edited, and rotated media to the top when sorting by modified date.
- **Organize albums by sections**: sorts albums into labeled, collapsible sections such as Common and Apps. **Manage sections** inside it lets you create, rename, delete, reorder, and show or hide sections; see [album organization](/ReFra/features/album-organization).
- **Show media type albums**: adds quick albums for all Videos, Photos, GIFs, and RAW files to the albums in Library.

## Display

- **Favorite icon on thumbnails**: where the heart badge sits on thumbnails: **Hidden**, or any of the four corners.
- **Date separators**: a switch per section (Timeline, Albums, Favorites, Vault, Cloud archive) controlling whether media is split under date headers.
- **Media grouping**: per section (Timeline, Albums, Favorites, Vault, Cloud archive), choose **Daily**, **Monthly**, or **Yearly** grouping. Changes apply across the app right away.

## Library

- **People**, **Location categories**, **Categories**: show or hide the matching section on the Library tab. The same switches appear in the Library's own edit mode; see [library](/ReFra/features/library).

## Example: clean up after a big import

You imported an old camera archive and everything landed under today's modified date:

1. Open **Settings › Timeline & Albums**.
2. Set **Timeline order** to **Capture time**, **Newest first**.
3. Tap **Recheck capture dates** so items whose dates were never read get placed correctly.
4. If albums look too crowded, set **Media grouping** for Timeline to **Monthly**.

## If an option seems to do nothing

- Grouping switches only affect items that actually match: RAW pairing needs both files to share a name and folder, and cloud+local pairing needs a connected cloud account.
- Sorting by **Date modified** is only useful with **Update modified date** expectations: a file you never touched keeps the modified date it arrived with.
- Section switches hide the section, not its content; a hidden People section does not delete any people groups.
