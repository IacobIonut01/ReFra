People groups photos by the faces in them. The grouping runs on your device with the face models installed; a connected provider such as Immich can also expose its own people groups. Either way the Library's **People** section lists them with a photo count.

## Scan and group

<PreviewCollection feature="people" section="scan-and-group" />

1. Open **Library › People**.
2. Tap **Scan for people** to run the first scan; progress shows "Scanning photos" with a counter, and you can cancel.
3. **Re-group faces** re-runs the grouping when new photos arrive or a merge went wrong.
4. Newly found groups appear under **New people**; tap **Add a name** to label one.

The **Named** and **New** filters narrow the list to people you named or groups waiting for a name.

Without the face grouping models, the screen offers **Set up face grouping** instead; download them under **Settings › Smart Features › Intelligence Hub › Face grouping**. See [AI models](/ReFra/features/ai-models).

## Work with a person

<PreviewCollection feature="people" section="work-with-a-person" />

Opening a person shows their photos, a date range, and their controls:

- **Edit name**: renames the person everywhere.
- **Add a birthday**: stores a birthday on the person.
- **Set cover**: pick the face thumbnail used for the person (tap the avatar). Available for on-device people.
- **Merge** / **Looks similar to**: when the app suspects two groups are the same person, it lists them under **Possible duplicates** on the list, or **Looks similar to** on the person page. Confirm with **Same person**, or reject with **Different**. Merging asks "Merge X into Y?" before combining.
- **Detected faces** (on-device people): the strip of individual faces. Touch and hold a face and choose **Remove face** to stop counting it as that person.
- **Remove from person** (on-device people): select photos in the person's grid and remove them from the group; the photos stay in your library, they just stop counting as this person.
- **Hide** / **Unhide**: hidden people move to a **Hidden** section; the list's **Hide hidden people** / **Show hidden people** controls whether that section is visible at all.
- **Blur everywhere**: obscures this person's face in every photo they appear in and saves the results as new copies; your originals are not overwritten.
- **Delete person**: removes the group and excludes their faces from future grouping. The confirmation is explicit: your photos are not deleted.

## People settings

**People settings** on the People screen holds two lists:

- **Sources**: which sources appear in People, **On this device** and each connected provider that has its own people.
- **Hidden people**: everyone you hid, with a count. Hidden people stay out of People and search.

## Local and cloud people

The controls above describe on-device people fully. On a cloud account, which actions exist depends on what that provider supports; selections on a cloud person fall back to the normal media actions instead of the face-specific ones.

## Example: fix a wrong merge

1. Two different friends got grouped as one person. Open that person.
2. Scroll to **Detected faces**, touch and hold each face that is the wrong friend, and tap **Remove face**.
3. If the faces form their own group, name it under **New people**.
4. If instead two existing people should be one, open either and use **Looks similar to** to merge; confirm with **Same person**.

## If people are missing

- The section needs the face grouping models; check the Intelligence Hub.
- A person you hid sits in the **Hidden** section; enable **Show hidden people** to see it.
- Scanning runs through **Smart Features data**; a scan marked Needs attention there explains why.
