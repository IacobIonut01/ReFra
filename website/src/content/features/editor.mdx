**Edit** in the viewer opens the ReFra editor by default. Photos open in the photo editor and videos in the [video editor](/ReFra/features/video-editor); both save a new copy and leave the original untouched.

The photo editor opens JPEG, PNG, and camera RAW files (DNG, CR2, CR3, NEF, NRW, ARW, RAF, ORF, RW2, PEF, and SRW). Other image formats open in the [built-in editor](/ReFra/features/legacy-editor) instead. To choose a different editor, open **Settings › Media Viewer** and change **Default image editor** or **Default video editor**; see [media viewer settings](/ReFra/features/viewer-settings).

Other apps can open the editor too: it accepts Android's edit request for JPEG and PNG photos and for videos, and returns the saved copy to the app that asked.

## The layout

<PreviewCollection feature="editor" section="the-layout" />

- **Header**: **Close**, **Undo**, **Redo**, **Compare**, and **Save**, which opens the save menu; see [saving copies](/ReFra/features/save-edits). Under it, the histogram shows the tones of the current preview.
- **Canvas**: the photo with your edits. Pinch to zoom and drag to pan. Touch and hold the photo to see the original; release to return to the edit.
- **Toolbar**: your pinned tools, **Colour**, and **Tools**, which opens the full catalogue.

Selecting a tool opens its controls above the toolbar. While the tool is open, **Save** becomes **Apply**, which adds the change to the recipe as one undo step, and **Reset** under the header clears what the tool changed. Selecting another tool applies the open one first; **Close** leaves the tool without its change.

**Compare** splits the canvas: the original sits left of a divider you can drag, the edit to its right. Once a crop, rotation, flip, or perspective change is in the recipe, the two no longer line up, so Compare shows the whole original instead.

## The tool catalogue

<PreviewCollection feature="editor" section="the-tool-catalogue" />

**Tools** opens every tool as a grid with a search field and four filters: **All**, **Adjust**, **Retouch**, and **Compose**.

| Group | Tools | Covered in |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Adjust | Light, Colour, Curves, White balance | [Light and colour](/ReFra/features/lighting-colour) |
| Adjust | Looks, Details, Vignette, Lens blur, Grain, Colour tone, Posterize, Edges, Borders | [Looks and effects](/ReFra/features/filters-effects) |
| Adjust | Develop (RAW files only) | [RAW development](/ReFra/features/raw-development) |
| Retouch | Markup | [Markup](/ReFra/features/markup) |
| Retouch | Cutout, Remove background | [Subject cutout](/ReFra/features/subject-cutout) |
| Compose | Double exposure, Transform, Blend and opacity | [Layers](/ReFra/features/editor-layers) |
| All only | Crop and straighten, Perspective | [Crop, straighten, and perspective](/ReFra/features/crop-rotate) |

The grid also holds **Layers**, **Source** (the original and its file details), **Edits** (every change in the recipe, with **Reset edits**), and **Settings**. **Cutout** and **Remove background** appear only once the Subject Cutout model is installed; see [AI models](/ReFra/features/ai-models).

## Pinned tools

<PreviewCollection feature="editor" section="pinned-tools" />

The toolbar starts with **Crop**, **Light**, **Markup**, and **Looks** pinned. In the catalogue, tap a tool's pin to add or remove it. The pin beside **All tools** opens **Edit pins**, where the arrows reorder the pinned list. You can pin up to four tools or the **Layers** workspace; **Colour** and **Tools** always stay on the toolbar.

## Editor settings

**Settings** in the catalogue opens the photo editor's own options:

- **Histogram**: the channel the histogram shows (RGB, red, green, blue, or luminance).
- **Show clipping**: marks crushed shadows and blown highlights on the canvas.
- **Show grid**: draws a grid while you straighten and correct perspective.
- **Pinned tools**: the toolbar list described above.
- **Copy format** and **RAW export**: where a saved copy goes and in which format; see [saving copies](/ReFra/features/save-edits).

## Leaving the editor

Back and **Close** first close an open tool, then the catalogue or panel. With unsaved changes, closing asks **Discard these edits?** with **Discard** or **Keep editing**. If the editor is closed while a copy is still saving, it asks before cancelling the export.

The editor keeps your recipe while it is open. If Android stops the app in the background, reopening the editor restores the photo and the edits you had committed.

## Example: straighten and brighten a photo

1. Open a crooked, underexposed photo and tap **Edit**.
2. Tap **Crop**, drag **Straighten** until the horizon is level, and tap **Apply**.
3. Tap **Light**, raise **Exposure** and **Shadows**, then tap **Apply**.
4. Touch and hold the photo to check it against the original.
5. Tap **Save**. The copy lands in `Pictures/ReFra` and the original stays as it was.
