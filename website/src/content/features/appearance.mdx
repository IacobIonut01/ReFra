Appearance controls how ReFra looks: its color palette, light and dark behavior, viewer effects, font, and launcher icon and name. None of these options touches your photos.

## Open Appearance

1. Open **Library** and tap the gear icon.
2. Tap **Appearance**.

## Theme

<PreviewCollection feature="appearance" section="theme" />

- **Light / Auto / Dark**: three circles under the **Theme** header set how the app follows brightness. **Light** and **Dark** force that mode; **Auto** follows the system setting. Each circle previews its own scheme, so the row always shows what the palette looks like in both modes.
- **Use Amoled Mode**: the row directly under the theme circles replaces dark grey surfaces with pure black, which saves a little battery on AMOLED screens. It only works while the app is in dark mode, so pick **Dark** or let **Auto** resolve to dark first.
- **Color palette**: pick the source of the app's accent colors. **Wallpaper colors** tints the interface to match your wallpaper. **Neutral** keeps a muted palette, and **Other colors** offers fixed accent colors to choose from.

## Visual effects

- **Fancy Blur**: blurs the background behind photos in the media viewer. Requires Android 12 or later; the row explains this when the device is too old.
- **Auto Contrast (Beta)**: keeps the viewer's buttons and icons readable over bright or dark parts of an image by adjusting their contrast, including the status bar. It changes the controls, not the image itself.
- **Animate media items**: plays a smooth zoom transition between a thumbnail and the full-screen viewer. Turn it off for instant, static transitions.

## Navigation

- **Classic navigation style**: replaces the floating dock (separate Timeline and Library tabs plus a search button) with a full-width Material bar that labels all three: **Timeline**, **Search**, and **Library**, in that order. On larger screens and windows it becomes a side rail rather than a bottom bar. Turn it off to return to the dock.
- **Hide navigation bar on scroll**: the bottom bar slides away while you scroll down and returns when you scroll up. The matching switch for the top bar, **Hide on scroll**, is under **Settings › Timeline & albums › Top bar**.
- **Gallery beside viewer**: on tablets, foldables, and other large windows, opening a photo from the Timeline keeps the gallery visible next to the viewer. Drag the divider to resize the panes, or double-tap it to restore the default size. Turn it off to always open photos fullscreen.

## Font

- **Use system font**: switches ReFra from its built-in font to your device's default font. Useful if you already set a more readable system font.

## App icon and name

- **App icon & name**: one page for both launcher choices. Under **Logo**, pick one of four icons: **ReFra**, **Gallery**, **Monochrome**, or **Gallery Mono**. Under **Name**, pick **ReFra** or **Gallery**; each name tile previews the icon you chose. Picks stay pending until you tap **Apply**; nothing changes earlier. The app then restarts once and the launcher shows the new icon and label.

## Map

- **Map appearance**: choose whether the location maps follow the app's theme or always use light or dark map colors: **Follow app appearance**, **Light**, or **Dark**.
- **CARTO basemap API key**: some builds need a personal key for map preview tiles; without one, tiles may be watermarked. Tap the row, paste the key the map service issued you, and tap **Save**. The row shows **Configured** or **Not configured** so you can tell the state at a glance.

## Example: a quiet dark setup

1. Under **Color palette**, choose **Neutral**.
2. Pick **Dark** in the theme row, then turn on **Use Amoled Mode**.
3. Turn off **Animate media items** for instant transitions.
4. Open a photo. The interface is black, muted, and still.

## If a switch is missing

- **Use Amoled Mode** waits until dark mode is active; pick **Dark** or let **Auto** follow a dark system theme first.
- **Fancy Blur** requires Android 12 or later.
- **CARTO basemap API key** only appears on builds that do not ship a bundled key.
