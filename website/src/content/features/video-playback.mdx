Videos open in the same [media viewer](/ReFra/features/media-viewer) as photos, with playback controls layered on top.

## Playing and seeking

<PreviewCollection feature="video-playback" section="playing-and-seeking" />

- Tap **Play video** / **Pause video** in the center of the controls. The position and total duration sit beside the seek bar.
- **Tap** the seek bar to jump to a position; **drag** along it to seek. While dragging, **drag upward** for finer horizontal control (the indicator shows the finer scale) then release. Releasing resumes playback only if the video was playing when you grabbed it.
- **Pinch** zooms into the video picture and **drag** pans it; **double-tap** toggles a 2.5x zoom, or resets if already zoomed.
- **Double-tap** the left or right side of an unzoomed video to skip 10 seconds back or forward.
- With **Tap sides to navigate** on in [viewer settings](/ReFra/features/viewer-settings), a side tap moves to the previous or next item. With **Instant side taps** on (the default), it moves at once and takes priority over the double-tap skip. With it off, a single side tap moves after a short wait and a side double tap still skips.

## When a video ends

With **Loop videos** on (the default), a video starts again from the beginning when it reaches the end. With it off, the video stops on its last frame and the controls come back; tap **Play video** to watch it again from the start. Slideshows always play each video once.

## More options

The **More options** button opens a small popup:

- **Subtitles**: opens a sheet listing the video's tracks plus **Off**. **Add subtitle file** attaches a track from storage through Android's picker, marked **External**, and **Remove subtitle track** drops it again.
- **Speed**: **Auto**, **0.125x**, **0.25x**, **0.5x**, **1x**, or **2x**.
- **Toggle audio** to mute or unmute, **Rotate Screen** to flip the view, **Close** to dismiss the popup.

## Related settings

In [media viewer settings](/ReFra/features/viewer-settings): **Hide UI on Video Play**, **Video Auto-Play**, **Loop videos**, **Fix black video on hide**, **HDR video playback**, **Tap sides to navigate**, and **Instant side taps** all shape this screen.

## Example: inspect one precise moment

1. Open the video and pause it.
2. Set **More options › Speed › 0.5x** so motion is easier to catch.
3. Drag on the seek bar, then drag upward for the fine scale to land on the exact frame.
4. If you want that frame as a photo, use [Extract frames](/ReFra/features/frame-extraction).
