**Settings › Gestures** gathers every touch interaction the [media viewer](/ReFra/features/media-viewer) responds to. For the full gesture map, including grids and the editor, see the [gesture reference](/ReFra/features/gesture-reference).

## Taps

<PreviewCollection feature="gestures" section="taps" />

- **Tap sides to navigate**: tapping the left or right edge of an unzoomed photo or video jumps to the previous or next item; tapping the center shows or hides the controls. Zoomed images, panoramas, slideshows, and locked or standalone viewers keep their usual gestures.
- **Instant side taps**: on by default, and only available while **Tap sides to navigate** is on. When on, a side tap changes the item straight away and takes priority over video seeking. When off, a single side tap changes the item only after the double-tap timeout, so a double tap on a side zooms a photo or skips a video 10 seconds back or forward instead. Taps on a zoomed photo or video never change the item.
- **Double-tap to zoom**: on by default. A double tap on a photo zooms in and out, and double-tap then drag zooms with one finger. Pinch to zoom always works, and videos keep their own double-tap gestures. When it is off, photo side taps respond immediately even with **Instant side taps** turned off, because nothing waits on a double tap.

## Swipe and hold

- **Swipe down to close**: on by default. Drag a photo or video down to return to the grid. The Back button and predictive back gesture are unaffected when it is off.
- **Long-press action**: picks what a long-press on an image does in the viewer: **Rotate** (the default), **Cut out subject**, or **None**. While the Subject Cutout model is not downloaded, **Cut out subject** is disabled with a hint to get it from AI models. You can always cut out a subject with the editor's **Cutout** tool; see [subject cutout](/ReFra/features/subject-cutout).

## Example: browse with taps only

1. Open **Settings › Gestures**.
2. Turn on **Tap sides to navigate** and leave **Instant side taps** on for the fastest paging.
3. Turn off **Double-tap to zoom** if you never zoom with double taps; pinch still works.
