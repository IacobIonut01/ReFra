Crop and rotation controls sit at the bottom of the [editor](/ReFra/features/legacy-editor), always reachable; no need to open a tool tab first.

## Crop

<PreviewCollection feature="legacy-crop-rotate" section="crop" />

1. Drag the corners of the crop frame to any shape (**Freeform**).
2. Or tap a preset ratio: **Square**, **5:4**, **4:3**, **3:2**, **16:9**, **4:5**, **3:4**, **2:3**, or **9:16**.
3. Toggle the grid guide on or off to line things up.
4. Everything outside the frame is cut when the edit is applied.

## Rotate and mirror

- **Rotate** turns the image 90 degrees at a time.
- A rotation scrubber fine-tunes the angle in degrees for small straightening fixes.
- **Mirror image** flips the picture horizontally.

**Undo** and **Redo** cover every step, and the crop applies with the rest of the edit when you save; there is no separate "resize by width and height" dialog; crop sets the output shape.

## Example: straighten a landscape, square a portrait

1. Open the tilted sea photo, tap **Edit**.
2. Use the rotate scrubber until the horizon sits level against the grid.
3. For a portrait post instead, pick the **4:5** preset and drag the frame over your subject.
4. **Save Copy** keeps the original framing untouched.
