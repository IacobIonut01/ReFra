**Settings › Media Viewer** holds the display and playback options for the full-screen photo and video view. Tap, swipe, and long-press controls live on the separate [Gestures](/ReFra/features/gestures) page, and editor and save-quality choices on the [Editors](/ReFra/features/editors) page.

## Media view

<PreviewCollection feature="viewer-settings" section="media-view" />

- **Full Brightness when viewing**: raises the screen to maximum brightness while a photo or video is open. Your normal brightness is restored when you leave the viewer.
- **Show Media Date Header**: puts the current item's date and time in the viewer's top bar.
- **Viewer action bar**: customizes the action chrome in the viewer. The slots **Next to Back** and **Next to Info**, beside the fixed **Back** and **Media Info** buttons, default to **Visual search** and **Cast**; each takes any quick action or can be left empty. The quick-actions pill under the media defaults to **Share**, **Copy to clipboard**, **Favorite**, **Open with**, **Restore**, **Download**, **Rename**, **Edit**, and **Trash**. Drag to reorder the bottom actions, and add or remove actions; an action appears in only one place and only when it applies to the current item. **Cast** is offered only in builds with network access, and **Favorite** needs Android 11 or later.
- **Disable smoothing**: draws images with crisp pixels when zoomed instead of a smoothed blur. Turn it on for pixel art and screenshots; leave it off for photos.
- **Dark viewer background**: keeps the viewer background black even in the light theme.
- **Editors**: opens the [Editors](/ReFra/features/editors) page, which picks the default image and video editors and the save quality.
- **Slideshow**: opens the slideshow options; see [slideshow](/ReFra/features/slideshow).

## Codes in photos

- **Read QR codes and barcodes**: on by default. The viewer reads codes in the photo you are viewing, on the device, and offers what they hold. Turned off, it never scans.
- **When to look**: **Automatically** scans each photo once you stop swiping; **Only when I tap Scan** adds a Scan button to the viewer's top bar instead.
- **Code types**: limits scanning to **QR codes**, **Other 2D codes**, **Product barcodes**, and **Other barcodes**.

See [codes in photos](/ReFra/features/codes-in-photos).

## Video playback

- **Hide UI on Video Play**: the top and bottom bars hide themselves a couple of seconds after a video starts; tap the screen to bring them back.
- **Video Auto-Play**: videos begin playing as soon as you swipe to them. Off means a manual tap to play.
- **Loop videos**: on by default. A video starts again from the beginning when it reaches the end. This applies to videos on the device, in the vault, and in cloud accounts. When it is off, a video stops on its last frame and the controls come back; **Play video** starts it again from the beginning. Slideshows always play each video once.
- **Fix black video on hide**: re-binds the video surface when controls hide or show, for devices where the picture otherwise goes black while audio continues. It is already enabled on devices where the issue is known.
- **HDR video playback**: switches the screen into HDR mode for Dolby Vision, HDR10, and HLG videos. It only works on an HDR-capable display and does nothing otherwise.
- **Visual search**: opens the handoff options for searching the current image with another app; see [visual search](/ReFra/features/visual-search).

## Example: crisp screenshots and quiet video

1. Open **Settings › Media Viewer**.
2. Turn on **Disable smoothing** so zoomed screenshots and pixel art stay sharp.
3. Turn off **Video Auto-Play** so videos wait for a tap.
4. Leave **Dark viewer background** on for a consistent black frame around media.

## If a control seems absent

- **HDR video playback** has no visible effect on a display that cannot do HDR.
- **Instant side taps** on the [Gestures](/ReFra/features/gestures) page stays greyed out until **Tap sides to navigate** is on.
- A prompt about side tapping that appears the first time the viewer opens is the same setting; **Enable** and **Keep off** map to it, and you can change it on the [Gestures](/ReFra/features/gestures) page later.
