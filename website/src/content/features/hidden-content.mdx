ReFra offers four ways to take media out of plain view, and they are not interchangeable. Picking the right one matters, because only one of them encrypts.

## The four tools

| Tool | What it does | Where it lives |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Ignored** | Rules that hide chosen albums or paths from the Timeline, Library, or both. Files stay exactly where they are. | Managed under **Library › Ignored**; see [ignored albums](/ReFra/features/ignored-albums). |
| **Locked albums** | An album stays visible but opening it asks for your device credential. Its cover stays concealed by default. | Lock and unlock from the album's long-press menu in Library. |
| **Private folder** | A designated folder whose contents leave the timeline and other apps' media listings. Files are still ordinary files on storage. | **Settings › Security › Private folder**; see [private folder](/ReFra/features/private-folder). |
| **Vault** | Real encryption: items are decrypted only while the vault is unlocked. | **Library › Vault**; see [vault](/ReFra/features/vault). |

A rough ladder: ignored tidies your own browsing, locked albums keep a casual viewer out, the private folder keeps files out of other gallery apps, and the vault protects contents even from someone reading the storage directly.

## Managing ignored content

<PreviewCollection feature="hidden-content" section="managing-ignored-content" />

1. Open **Library** and tap **Ignored**.
2. The screen lists every active ignore rule with where it applies and what it matches.
3. Tap a rule to change its albums, pattern, or where it hides them, or tap **Stop ignoring**; removing a rule brings the albums back and never touches files.
4. A separate way in: touch and hold an album in Library and choose **Add to ignored albums**, which starts the same steps.

The rule wizard itself (Timeline vs Albums scope, single albums, multiple albums, and path patterns) is covered on [ignored albums](/ReFra/features/ignored-albums).

## Ignored content and smart features

A switch under **Settings › Smart Features**, **Include ignored albums**, decides whether timeline-hidden media still feeds Smart Features and search. Turning it on starts a full refresh; locked albums stay private regardless.

## If something is more visible or less visible than expected

- A folder gone from the Timeline but still in Albums (or the reverse) is an ignore rule scoped to one location; check its entry under **Library › Ignored**.
- A locked album that keeps asking is working as designed; the credential prompt is the feature.
- None of these is a substitute for caution with deletion: hiding never deletes files, and deleting a hidden item still deletes it.

## Example: decluttered screenshots, protected scans

1. `Screenshots` clutters the timeline but you still want it under Albums: touch and hold it, choose **Add to ignored albums**, and scope the rule to **Timeline**.
2. Passport scans must stay unreadable even to someone browsing the files: select them in their album and use **Hide** to move them into a vault; see [vault](/ReFra/features/vault).
3. Confirm the result in **Library › Ignored** for the rule and the **Vault** entry for the scans.
4. Undoing either is non-destructive: delete the ignore rule, or restore the scans out of the vault.
