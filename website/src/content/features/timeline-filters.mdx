The filter icon in the timeline's top-right control group opens the **Timeline controls** sheet. It combines sorting and filtering in one place: pick what to show, pick the order, tap **Apply**.

## Open the sheet

1. Go to the **Timeline** tab.
2. Tap the filter icon beside the overflow menu.
3. Choose the options you need; sections below.
4. Tap **Apply**. The grid reloads with only matching items.
5. To clear everything, open the sheet again and tap **Reset**.

A small dot appears on the slider icon while a filter or a non-default sort is active, so you can tell at a glance that the timeline is narrowed.

## Options

<PreviewCollection feature="timeline-filters" section="options" />

| Section | Choices | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Order by | **Capture time**, **Date modified** | Capture time uses the date embedded in the photo or video, with the modified time as a fallback. Date modified uses when the file was last changed. |
| Direction | **Newest first**, **Oldest first** | Reverses the sort direction for the chosen date. |
| Type | **All**, **Photos**, **Videos** | Limits the grid to one media type. |
| Status | **Favorites** | Shows only items you have marked with a heart. |
| Time | One chip per year present in the library | Shows only items from the tapped year or years. |
| Source | One chip per filterable album | Shows only items in the tapped album or albums. Merged albums include every folder they merged. Locked and cloud-internal folders are not offered. |

Sections combine: choosing **Videos**, the year 2025, and the album **Camera** shows only videos from 2025 that live in Camera. Nothing applies until you tap **Apply**, so you can set several sections in one pass.

While ReFra is still checking embedded capture dates, the sheet shows a "Checking capture dates" progress line. Ordering may shift as dates are read; files without an embedded date keep sorting by their modified time.

## Example: favorite videos from 2025 in Camera

1. On the Timeline, tap the slider icon.
2. Under **Type**, choose **Videos**.
3. Under **Status**, choose **Favorites**.
4. Under **Time**, tap **2025**.
5. Under **Source**, tap **Camera**.
6. Tap **Apply**. The grid now holds only favorited videos recorded in 2025 that sit in the Camera album.
7. When done, reopen the sheet and tap **Reset**. The full timeline returns.

## If a filter seems to hide too much

- Filters add up: an item must match every active section. If the grid is unexpectedly empty, tap **Reset** and apply one section at a time.
- A device folder that does not appear under **Source** is probably covered by an ignore rule or locked. Check the rules under **Library › Ignored** (see [ignored albums](/ReFra/features/ignored-albums)) or unlock it first.
- Filtering is a view, not a change. Nothing is moved, hidden, or deleted when you apply or reset it.
