The filter icon in the timeline's top-right control group opens the **Timeline controls** sheet. It combines sorting and filtering in one place. There is no Apply button: each filter takes effect as soon as you tap it.

## Open the sheet

1. Go to the **Timeline** tab.
2. Tap the filter icon beside the overflow menu.
3. Choose the options you need; sections below. The grid behind the sheet narrows with every filter chip you tap.
4. Close the sheet by swiping it down or pressing back. A new order takes effect at that point, and the timeline returns to the top.
5. To clear everything, open the sheet again and tap **Reset**.

A small dot appears on the filter icon while a filter or a non-default sort is active, so you can tell at a glance that the timeline is narrowed.

[Favorites](/ReFra/features/favorites) and every album have the same sheet, titled **Sort and filter**; each keeps its own filter and its own order.

## Options

<PreviewCollection feature="timeline-filters" section="options" />

| Section | Choices | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Order by | **Capture time**, **Date modified** | Capture time uses the date embedded in the photo or video, with the modified time as a fallback. Date modified uses when the file was last changed. |
| Direction | **Newest first**, **Oldest first** | Reverses the sort direction for the chosen date. |
| Type | **All**, **Photos**, **Videos** | Limits the grid to one media type. |
| Status | **Favorites** | Shows only items you have marked with a heart. |
| Time | One chip per year present in the library | Shows only items from the tapped year or years. |
| Source | One chip per filterable album | Shows only items in the tapped album or albums. Merged albums include every folder they merged. Locked and cloud-internal folders are not offered. |
| Remember filters | On or off (off by default) | Timeline only. Keeps the current filters when you leave the timeline or close ReFra, until you change them or tap **Reset**. |

Sections combine: choosing **Videos**, the year 2025, and the album **Camera** shows only videos from 2025 that live in Camera. Filters belong to the screen you set them on, and the order is always saved. On the timeline, filters reset when you leave it unless **Remember filters** is on. A remembered album that no longer exists is dropped from the filter, and the dot on the filter icon clears once nothing else is set.

While ReFra is still checking embedded capture dates, the sheet shows a "Checking capture dates" progress line. Ordering may shift as dates are read; files without an embedded date keep sorting by their modified time.

## Example: favorite videos from 2025 in Camera

1. On the Timeline, tap the filter icon.
2. Under **Type**, choose **Videos**.
3. Under **Status**, choose **Favorites**.
4. Under **Time**, tap **2025**.
5. Under **Source**, tap **Camera**. The grid now holds only favorited videos recorded in 2025 that sit in the Camera album.
6. Swipe the sheet down to look at them.
7. When done, reopen the sheet and tap **Reset**. The full timeline returns.

## Example: keep screenshots out of the timeline

Two ways work, depending on whether you want the rest of the library filtered too:

- **Hide one folder for good**: add an ignore rule for Screenshots under **Library › Ignored** and set **Hide from** to **Timeline**. The album stays in Library; see [ignored albums](/ReFra/features/ignored-albums).
- **Show only chosen folders**: in the sheet, tap **Camera** (and any other album you want) under **Source**, then turn on **Remember filters**. The timeline keeps showing only those albums after you leave it.

## If a filter seems to hide too much

- Filters add up: an item must match every active section. If the grid is unexpectedly empty, tap **Reset** and set one section at a time.
- A device folder that does not appear under **Source** is probably covered by an ignore rule or locked. Check the rules under **Library › Ignored** (see [ignored albums](/ReFra/features/ignored-albums)) or unlock it first.
- Filtering is a view, not a change. Nothing is moved, hidden, or deleted when you set or reset it.
