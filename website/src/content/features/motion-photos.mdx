A motion photo is a still photo with a short video clip embedded inside the same file; the camera recorded a second or two around the shutter press. ReFra marks these with a **Motion** pill on the viewer's top bar; it only appears on photos that actually contain a clip.

## In the viewer

<PreviewCollection feature="motion-photos" section="in-the-viewer" />

- Tap the **Motion** pill to play or stop the embedded clip over the still.
- A **Shots in this photo** strip can appear along the bottom, letting you scrub through the individual frames the clip contains.
- Zooming, paging, and the rest of the viewer gestures work as on any photo; see [media viewer](/ReFra/features/media-viewer).

## Keep a piece of it

- **More options › Save as video** writes the embedded clip out as a separate video file, leaving the original photo untouched.
- **More options › Extract frames** opens the [frame picker](/ReFra/features/frame-extraction) to save chosen frames as standalone photos.

## Edits and the still

The still picture and the embedded clip are distinct parts of one file. Editing the photo in the ReFra editor saves a new copy of the still and leaves the motion photo intact; see [saving copies](/ReFra/features/save-edits).

## Example: rescue the better moment

1. A group shot blinked at the shutter. Open it and tap the **Motion** pill; the moments just before and after play.
2. Scrub the **Shots in this photo** strip to the frame where everyone has eyes open.
3. Use **Extract frames**, save that frame as its own photo, and share the new still instead of the original.
