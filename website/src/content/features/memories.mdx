ReFra collects highlights into story cards above the timeline grid. One card fills the available phone width; two sit side by side without cropping. Larger sets use a carousel with smaller previews beside the prominent card. A quick swipe can move through several cards before settling. Titles sit over a width-matched scrim, and an icon at the top left identifies the card type. Tap a card to open a full-screen story that plays through the photos on its own.

## The cards

<PreviewCollection feature="memories" section="the-cards" />

Different card types surface different slices of the library:

- **Memories**: photos taken on this day in earlier years.
- **Albums**: highlighted and pinned albums.
- **Categories**: AI-classified groups, when the models are installed.
- **Locations**: photos grouped by where they were taken.
- **Favorites**: your hearted shots.
- **Cloud Memories**: "on this day" memories pulled from a connected cloud server.

Cards only appear when they have something to show. No cards means no highlights right now.

## The story viewer

<PreviewCollection feature="memories" section="the-story-viewer" />

Tap any card to open it full screen.

- **Watch**: a segmented progress bar at the top tracks position through the items; each slide auto-advances when its time is up. Videos play through and move on when they end.
- **Tap left third** for the previous item; **tap right third** for the next.
- **Touch and hold** pauses the story while your finger is down; release resumes. A pause button at the top does the same and stays paused.
- **Swipe down** closes the story and drops you back on the timeline.
- **Bottom row**: share or favorite the current item without leaving the story.

## Choose which cards appear

1. Open **Settings › Timeline & Albums › Story cards**.
2. Switch **Show Story Cards** off to remove the row entirely.
3. Under **Card types & order**, toggle each type and reorder them by dragging the handle on a card row, or with the **Move up / Move down** actions on its menu.
4. Under **Story viewer**, toggle **Auto-advance** and set **Duration per photo** (between 3 and 10 seconds).

## Example: a five-year flashback

1. Open the Timeline on a day that has a **Memories** card; it collects shots from this day in earlier years.
2. Tap the card. The story plays through the set.
3. When a favorite shot comes up, tap the pause button at the top, tap the heart to mark it, then tap play to resume.
4. Swipe down when it ends; you are back on the timeline where you left it.

## If no cards show

- There may simply be no highlights yet. The row only appears when a card has content.
- The row can be switched off: check **Settings › Timeline & Albums › Story cards › Show Story Cards**.
- Categories and Cloud Memories need the AI models or a connected cloud account.
