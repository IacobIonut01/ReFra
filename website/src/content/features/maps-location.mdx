Photos and videos that carry GPS coordinates appear under **Library › Locations**, on a map of everywhere you have shot and as a list of places. Showing coordinates inside the app needs the **media location access** permission; the app reads the locations stored in your photos; it does not do live GPS tracking. Grant it from the prompt the Locations card shows, or in Android's app settings.

## Places

<PreviewCollection feature="maps-location" section="places" />

**Library › Locations** opens on the map. The places button in the map's toolbar swaps in the places list, grouped by city and country; back returns to the map. Builds without an interactive map show the places list directly.

The banner at the top counts how many photos have a location and in how many places and countries; tap it to **open the map**. The filters switch between **All**, **Cities**, and **Countries**, and **Recent trips** appears when your photos include trips. Tap a place for its photos, with a header showing the years it covers and a **Map** button.

Without the permission, the list explains why it is needed and offers to grant it; the location stays on the phone and is never uploaded. With no geotagged photos yet, it reads **No places yet**.

## Using the map

<PreviewCollection feature="maps-location" section="using-the-map" />

- **Pan** and **pinch** to move and zoom; the usual map gestures apply.
- Nearby markers group into numbered clusters. Tapping a cluster zooms until the items separate; when every item shares the spot or you reach maximum zoom, a **Photos in this location** sheet opens with the grid.
- Tapping a marker scrolls the grid to that photo; scrolling the grid moves the selection on the map.
- Tap a photo in the grid to open it in the viewer; the sheet's place header opens a per-location timeline.
- The toolbar's appearance icon switches the map between **Follow app**, **Light**, and **Dark**. If tiles fail, **Couldn't load map** offers **Retry**.

On builds without an interactive map (the offline flavor has no map tiles), Locations still works as a browsable list of places. In the viewer, a photo's **Location** row opens a detail sheet with **Show in app** and **Open externally**; the external option needs a map app installed, and on a build without the map it is the direct route.

There is no in-app coordinate editing; to strip location entirely, use [remove metadata](/ReFra/features/metadata-privacy).

Some builds show a **CARTO API key** row in [appearance settings](/ReFra/features/appearance); it only appears where no key is embedded, and it affects the map's style data, not your photos.

## Example: revisit the city trip

1. Grant media location access, then open **Library › Locations** and open the map.
2. Zoom into the city cluster and tap it until the **Photos in this location** sheet opens.
3. Scroll the grid while the map follows, tap a photo to view it, or tap the place header to browse that location's whole timeline.
