**Sync** means fetching the server's current list of media and albums so the timeline and album views show what is actually there. It is about *listings and metadata*, not file contents; a synced item can still be remote-only. Uploading local photos is [backup](/ReFra/features/cloud-backup); fetching originals is [downloads](/ReFra/features/cloud-downloads).

## Sync controls

Per account, under its settings:

- **Sync now**: fetch the latest media and album listing immediately; shows **Syncing…** while running. It works on whatever network is currently available; it is not blocked off Wi-Fi.
- **Auto-sync**: periodically sync media and albums. The **Sync interval** choices are **Every 15 minutes**, **Every 30 minutes**, **Every hour**, **Every 6 hours**, **Every 12 hours**, or **Every 24 hours**; the interval is approximate since Android schedules background work around battery and other apps.
- **Wi-Fi only**: restricts the automatic syncs to Wi-Fi connections; it does not stop a manual **Sync now**.
- On the **Backup & Sync** dashboard (**Library › Backup & Sync**), **Sync when app opens** checks for new device photos and remote changes every time the app starts.

## Sync status

Sync progress shows on the **Backup & Sync** dashboard (**Library › Backup & Sync**). Its summary card reports a running sync or status check, and offers **Retry** when an account is in error. Synced items are listings, not files: a remote-only item stays on the server until you download it, as described in [cloud downloads](/ReFra/features/cloud-downloads).

## Remote deletions

Under **Advanced**, **Sync remote deletions** is an opt-in: when a file disappears on the server, the app deletes the copies it downloaded. It does not touch arbitrary photos on the phone, only files the app itself fetched.

## Example: keep a home server fresh

1. Your Immich account at home adds photos from other devices through the day.
2. In its settings, enable **Auto-sync** at **Every hour** with **Wi-Fi only** on.
3. The timeline picks up the new server photos as listings; you see them without downloading any originals.
