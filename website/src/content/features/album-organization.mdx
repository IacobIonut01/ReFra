The albums in **Library** can grow to dozens of folders. ReFra gives you four tools to keep it tidy: pinning, custom covers, merging, and grouping. None of them moves or renames a single file on storage.

## Pin an album

<PreviewCollection feature="album-organization" section="pin-an-album" />

Pinning keeps an album in the carousel above the other albums.

1. Touch and hold the album, **Camera** for example.
2. Tap **Pin**. The album joins the pinned row.
3. To remove it, touch and hold it again and tap **Unpin**.

## Change an album's cover

Albums show their most recent item as the cover by default. To choose your own:

1. Open the album.
2. Touch and hold the photo you want as the cover.
3. Tap **Use as album cover** in the action sheet.
4. To go back to the automatic cover, touch and hold the album in **Library** and tap **Remove custom album cover**.

## Merge albums and subfolders

Two different merges exist, and they solve different problems:

- **Merge albums by name**: a setting under **Settings › Timeline & Albums**. If "Camera" exists on both internal storage and an SD card, they appear as one Camera album. Filtering the timeline by a merged album includes every source folder.
- **Merge subfolders**: a per-album action. Touch and hold an album and choose **Merge subfolders** to fold its subfolders into one album view, or **Merge subfolders of parent folder** to merge at the next level up. **Unmerge subfolders** restores the split view.

## Groups and sections

**Album groups** are named bundles you create yourself: "Trips", "Receipts", anything. They appear as collapsible entries among the albums.

1. Touch and hold an album and tap **Add to group**.
2. Choose **Create new group** and type a name, or pick an existing group.
3. Touch and hold a group to **Rename group**, **Edit group**, or **Delete group**. Deleting a group never deletes the albums inside.
4. To take an album out, touch and hold it and tap **Remove from group**.

**Album sections** are automatic categories (Common, Apps, Other) that sort albums by where they are stored. Enable them under **Settings › Timeline & Albums**, then:

- Tap a section header to collapse or expand it.
- Touch and hold an album and tap **Move to section** to override where it sits; the app remembers your choice.

## Media-type albums

The **Media types** section at the bottom of the tab holds virtual albums for all photos, all videos, all GIFs, and all RAW files, across every folder at once. Show or hide the section under **Settings › Timeline & Albums**.

## Create an album

The album picker that appears when copying or moving media includes a **New album** tile. Type a name and check the **Location** line before tapping **Confirm**: it shows where the folder will be created, and the locations offered depend on the storage access Android has granted the app.

## Example: tidy a cluttered album list

1. In **Albums**, touch and hold **Camera** and tap **Pin**. Repeat for **Screenshots** so both stay in the pinned row.
2. Touch and hold **Holiday 2024**, tap **Add to group**, create a group called **Trips**, and add **Holiday 2023** to the same group.
3. Enable sections in **Settings › Timeline & Albums** and collapse **Apps** so app folders stop filling the screen.
4. The albums now show your two pinned albums, a compact Trips group, and far less noise. Nothing moved on disk.

## If a section or group misbehaves

- Groups and sections are presentation only; deleting a group or moving an album between sections never touches files.
- An album placed in the wrong automatic section can be reassigned with **Move to section**.
- Albums covered by an ignore rule for albums disappear from this screen. Manage the rules under **Library › Ignored**; see [ignored albums](/ReFra/features/ignored-albums).
