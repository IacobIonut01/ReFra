**Edit** on a video opens the ReFra video editor. It works on MP4 and QuickTime clips and always exports a new copy, so the original video stays untouched. To have **Edit** open another app or ask each time, change **Default video editor** in [media viewer settings](/ReFra/features/viewer-settings).

## The layout

<PreviewCollection feature="video-editor" section="the-layout" />

- **Header**: **Close**, **Undo**, **Redo**, and **Export**.
- **Preview and transport**: the current frame, the time and total length, **Previous frame**, **Play**, **Next frame**, and a sound toggle for the preview. Muting the preview does not remove sound from the export.
- **Timeline**: the clips on their tracks, with **Trim**, **Split**, **Delete**, and **Arrange** above them and zoom buttons (**Fit** shows the whole sequence). Tap a clip to select it, drag the time ruler to seek, and use **+** beside the tracks to add a video or audio track.
- **Toolbar**: **Media**, **Layers**, **Audio**, and **Tools**.

**Tools** opens every tool, grouped as **Edit**, **Picture**, **Audio**, and **Deliver**, with a search field.

## Cut and arrange clips

<PreviewCollection feature="video-editor" section="cut-and-arrange-clips" />

- **Trim** sets a clip's **In point** and **Out point**. Drag the handles at the clip's ends, or use the buttons to move either end one frame at a time. **Reset trim** restores the full clip.
- **Split** cuts the selected clip at the playhead; **Delete** removes it.
- **Arrange clips** moves the selected clip **Earlier** or **Later**, can **Duplicate** it, and has two switches: **Ripple later clips**, so inserting shifts later clips along, and **Link audio to its video**.
- Drag a clip to another position or onto another track. **Snap while dragging** in the editor's settings lines dropped clips up with the track start and clip edges.

If you delete every clip, the editor shows **Add your first clip**; add media or undo to bring the last clip back.

## Add media and overlays

**Media** (or **Add media** in the catalogue) adds another video from the system picker:

- **Insert at the playhead** splits the sequence there and moves later clips along.
- **Add to the end** places it after the last clip.
- **Add as an overlay** puts it on a layer above the main clips.

**Layers** lists the main video and its overlay layers. Each can be shown, hidden, or locked, and **Add overlay** adds another layer.

## Picture

<PreviewCollection feature="video-editor" section="picture" />

Picture tools change only the selected clip; other clips are unchanged.

- **Transform & crop** sets the **Content mode** (**Fit**, **Fill**, or **Crop**), **Scale**, **Rotation**, and **X** and **Y** position, with **Lock proportions**, **Flip horizontally**, **Centre**, and a **Background** for empty areas.
- **Clip colour** has **Light**, **Colour**, and **Curves** tabs, plus **Opacity**. **Reset clip edits** clears the clip's picture changes.

## Export

<PreviewCollection feature="video-editor" section="export" />

**Export** opens a summary of exactly what will be written: the size, a constant frame rate, H.264 video in an MP4 file, an automatic bit rate, stereo 48 kHz AAC audio when any sound reaches the mix, and a new copy as the destination. **Export video** starts it; the sheet shows progress and can be hidden while it runs.

The copy is saved to `Movies/ReFra`, and a notification tracks it; see [saving copies](/ReFra/features/save-edits). If an export stops, **Try again** retries it and your edits stay as they were.

## Editor settings

<PreviewCollection feature="video-editor" section="editor-settings" />

**Settings** in the video editor holds:

- **Snap while dragging**: dropped clips land on the lane start and clip edges.
- **Ripple later clips**: inserting shifts later clips along.
- **Fill empty audio tracks**: new audio goes to the selected empty track; see [video audio](/ReFra/features/video-audio).
- **Microphone**: whether the editor may record voice-overs.
- **Export**: the format, resolution, frame rate, and audio the exporter writes.

## Example: cut a clip down

1. Open the video and tap **Edit**.
2. Play to the first moment you want to keep and tap **Split**; select the part before it and tap **Delete**.
3. Select the remaining clip, tap **Trim**, and move its **Out point** in.
4. Tap **Export**, then **Export video**.
