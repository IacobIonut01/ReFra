Most of these tools sit in the **Adjust** group of the catalogue. Like every tool, each opens its controls above the toolbar and adds its change as one undo step when you tap **Apply**.

## Looks

<PreviewCollection feature="filters-effects" section="looks" />

**Looks** applies a finished colour treatment. The filters along the top narrow the grid: **All**, **Colour**, **Cinematic**, **Film**, **B&W**, and **My presets**. Each look shows a preview of your photo; **Original** removes the look. After picking one, **Strength** sets how much of it applies.

To keep your own settings, tap **New preset**. A preset saves the current look with your **Light** and **Colour** settings, and it appears under **My presets**. The options on a saved preset can **Update with current**, **Rename**, or **Delete** it. Your presets stay available the next time you open the editor.

## Details and lens blur

<PreviewCollection feature="filters-effects" section="details-and-lens-blur" />

- **Details**: **Texture**, **Clarity**, **Sharpening**, and **Noise reduction**.
- **Lens blur**: a **Radial** or **Linear** blur that keeps the focus area sharp. Drag the blur centre on the photo, then set **Strength**, **Transition**, and **Highlight bokeh**; **Linear** also has an **Angle**.

## Vignette and grain

- **Vignette**: **Amount**, **Midpoint**, **Feather**, and **Roundness**, plus **Highlights**, which protects bright tones from a darkening vignette. Negative amounts darken the edges; positive amounts lighten them.
- **Grain**: **Amount**, **Size**, and **Roughness** for a film-like texture.

## Colour tone

**Colour tone** has single-purpose colour moves the Colour tool does not cover: **Skin tone** shifts the red-green balance, **Blue tone** the blue channel, **Hue** rotates every colour, and **Black & white** removes colour by the amount you set.

## Posterize, edges, and borders

<PreviewCollection feature="filters-effects" section="posterize-edges-and-borders" />

**Posterize**, **Edges**, and **Borders** share one tool with a tab for each:

- **Posterize** reduces the number of tonal levels.
- **Edges** blends the photo toward an outline drawing of itself.
- **Borders** paints a frame inside the edges with a **Border width** and a colour from the swatch row.

## Example: a soft film look

1. Open **Looks**, filter to **Film**, and pick a look; lower **Strength** a little.
2. Open **Grain** and add a small **Amount**.
3. Finish with a light **Vignette**.
4. Tap **New preset** in **Looks** so the same treatment is one tap away next time.
