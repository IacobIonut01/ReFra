## What's new in 5.2.2
ReFra 5.2.2 makes the Library fully yours with editable sections and proper people management, dresses up photo widgets with black & white and icon styles, plays HDR video for real on compatible displays, and lets you choose whether deletes hit the device, the cloud, or both — plus a hidden developer menu with session logs and a large round of fixes.

### New features
- **Editable Library sections** — long-press any Library section header or item to enter edit mode and toggle sections; matching switches live under Settings → Timeline & Albums.
- **Hidden people & person deletion** — the People section keeps an "N hidden" entry, and a person can be unhidden or permanently deleted without resurfacing on rescan.
- **Widget display styles** — photo widgets render in colour, black & white, or as an icon/emoji stand-in, with a live preview while configuring.
- **Widgets open the tapped photo** — tapping a media widget opens that exact photo in the media viewer.
- **HDR video playback** — Dolby Vision, HDR10 and HLG videos play in HDR on compatible displays via a new media-viewer setting.
- **Delete from device, cloud, or both** — backed-up items offer a scope picker with a remembered default, also in Settings → General.
- **Sync when app opens** — an optional sync/download pass on every cold start, on the Backup & Sync dashboard.
- **Immich album management** — shared albums merge into the list; owned albums can be renamed, shared, deleted or have members removed.
- **Developer menu & session logs** — hold the Settings version footer to unlock anonymized session logs, breadcrumbs, crash capture and report/export.

### Improvements
- **Capture-date ordering for cloud** — WebDAV, Nextcloud, ownCloud, SMB and NFS media sort by embedded capture time, not server modified date.
- **Persistent heavy-format thumbnails** — JXL, PSD, JP2, TIFF, RAW and deep HEIF thumbnails are stored on disk instead of re-decoding every launch.
- **Haze 2.0 frosted surfaces** — all blur surfaces migrated to haze 2.0, fixing transparent bars on empty grids.
- **Instant viewer when animations are off** — "Animate media items" now gates every viewer animation, and the exit overlay no longer swallows taps.

### Bug fixes
- Vault decrypted temps are shared per file — no more multi-GB cache growth — and a failed add can't delete the original.
- Trash works on mixed local + cloud selections; providers without a bin are clearly marked.
- Preset crop ratios commit against the settled cropper — Save Copy no longer stuck disabled.
- Video details recovered when the isolated parser wedges on Android 12.
- Redacted GPS shows as absent and re-parses once media-location access is granted.
- Trash, delete and favorite use MediaStore request APIs — fixed on GrapheneOS/Android 17.
- Immich downloads land in account/album folders; identical local files are linked, not re-downloaded.
- Immich v3 and shared albums open fully populated.
- Album covers fall back when their photo is deleted.
- Person merges commit, and rescans keep people names.
- "Delete permanently" labels shortened; shortcut editing stays reachable when all tiles are hidden.
- 12-bit AVIF/HEIF files decode correctly through the bundled software decoder.
